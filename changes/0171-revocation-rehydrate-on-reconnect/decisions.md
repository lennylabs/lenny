# Decisions: 0171 Revocation cache rehydration on event-bus reconnect

## Open decisions

None.

## Decisions

1. **Each replica rehydrates its revocation cache when its event-bus subscription reconnects, and §13.3 states the bound (FM-30).** Each gateway replica rehydrates its revocation cache immediately when its event-bus subscription reconnects, or when the bus reports a dropped message. §13.3 states the bound: a lost `token.revoked` event is reconciled by the next rehydration, within 30 seconds, and a replica refuses validation after 90 seconds without a successful rehydration. The per-request Postgres read is removed from the text, and no validation reads the database. Owner's answer to FM-30 on the M3 readiness decision page and in chat, 2026-11-23, which settles open decision 14 of change 0169 (a concurrency model of the token rules, [decision 14](../archive/2026-11-23-0169-token-concurrency-model/decisions.md)) with an option of its own and lands it as this change rather than as an issue. The options not chosen were a per-token Postgres read behind a short TTL cache (default 5 seconds), a specification-only edit describing the current code (0169's option (b)), and an issue under TOKX for the missing read (0169's option (a)).
2. **Rehydration on every subscription confirmation, the first included (RH-1).** The bus calls the reconnect callback on every subscription confirmation for the channel, the first one included, so a replica whose first subscription is confirmed after its startup rehydration began applies a revocation published in between at once rather than at the next periodic rehydration. RR-1 and §13.3 are revised to match. The extra rehydration at startup is coalesced by the trigger. Owner's answer to RH-1 on the M3 readiness decision page, 2026-11-25, option (a). The option not chosen was (b), keeping RR-1 as written, with the startup case under the 30-second periodic bound. The review of 0171.S1 (general finding 3, security finding 4) raised it.
3. **A minimum spacing between reconnect-triggered rehydrations (RH-2).** A reconnect-triggered rehydration starts no sooner than a minimum spacing after the previous reconnect-triggered rehydration started, default 5 seconds, overridable by a gateway flag. No metric is added. The periodic rehydration keeps its 30-second interval, so the spacing bounds the load of a flapping Redis connection at one read per replica per spacing and delays a reconnect's rehydration by at most the spacing, inside the 30-second bound. Owner's answer to RH-2 on the M3 readiness decision page, 2026-11-25, option (c). The options not chosen were (a), no spacing, and (b), the spacing with a counter of reconnect-triggered rehydrations in the §16.1 metrics catalog. The review of 0171.S1 (security finding 3) raised it.
