5. **The upload secret in the adapters of change 0138 (O5).** An adapter is a client of the REST API like any other. It generates or derives the upload secret and keeps it, by a means that is the adapter's own concern, sends the digest, presents the secret on upload and finalize, and never returns the secret to its own clients. Lenny prescribes no derivation, key, storage, or replay behaviour for an adapter, and adds no response header for it. The adapter's clients do not hold the secret; an upload or a finalize that reaches the gateway through an adapter is authorized for them by their bearer and the route's ownership check, and by the secret the adapter presents. 0138's records state the adapter's duty under "RO-8" (decision 7). The owner answered UC-5 on the M3 readiness decision page on 2026-11-27 with option (a), after the option's text was rewritten on the page following a discussion in chat. The text answered reads: "(a) The adapter acts as any other client: it generates or derives the secret and keeps it (how is its own concern), sends the digest, presents the secret on upload and finalize, and never returns it to its own clients." The owner's words in chat, 2026-11-27: "the adapter should store or derive the key and secret on its own, just like any other client." Not chosen: the option (a) of revision 2, under which the adapter derived the secret by HKDF-SHA256 from its adapter key, the tenant, the subject, its registration, and the idempotency key, required the adapter key for uploads before start, and either returned the secret on a replayed create (a1) or withheld it on a replay marked by a response header `Idempotency-Replayed: true` (a2); (b), exposing `uploadTokenDigest` as a tool argument that the client's host computes; and (c), an adapter that offers no upload before start.
