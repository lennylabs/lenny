# Decisions: 0165 A formal model of the token rules

## Open decisions

None. The owner settled every open decision of this change on 2026-11-22 on the M3 readiness decision page, recorded below as decisions 2 to 12. Decisions 13 and 14 record the owner's answers on where the fixes land and on the model's next steps.

## Decisions

Each decision from 2 to 12 answers a counterexample under "Counterexamples" in `change.md` and names the change that carries the rule change. Change 0164 (token exchange narrowing and identity rules) carries the answers to CX-2 and CX-13, which were its own open decisions O3 and O4. Change 0166, a new change that carries the fixes for the model's other findings under decision 13, carries the rest. CX-1 needed no decision, because 0164.S1 closes it. CX-12 needed no decision either: it is the residual that 0164 decision 8 accepted when it required a child of another user's subject to be strictly narrower and rejected a second lineage edge from the subject, and the model shows that the same residual holds for a subject of the actor's own `sub`, with the child's `exp` bounded by the subject's.

1. **Whether to build a formal model of the token rules (owner's request of 2026-11-20).** Three review passes over changes 0162, 0163, and 0164 found about ten design gaps in token exchange, narrowing, revocation, and impersonation (TOKX-I2 to TOKX-I11, and open decisions O1 and O2 of 0164). The owner asked on 2026-11-20 for a formal model that makes the design of those rules sound, and agreed the plan of two steps: 0165.S1 states the properties in plain words and checks them with an Alloy model, and 0165.S2 checks the same properties against the Token Service with generated property tests in Go. A TLA+ model of the concurrent orderings is held as a candidate until the owner has seen the Alloy results. The change is outside program v1, in the manner of 0161 to 0164.
2. **CX-2 Whether a rotation records the token it replaces (0164 O3, SM-12).** Settled by the owner on 2026-11-22, choosing option (a): restore the rotation edge, so the token a rotation issues records the token it replaces as its parent, and state in the operator security guide that a leak of more than one token of a user is answered with a full revoke of the user. Change 0164 carries it as its decision 10, which amends its decision 7 and removes the inheritance of the replaced token's `parent_jti` and `act_sub`; 0164.S1 built it, and the security page states the full-revoke advice. The model's `RotEdge` variant is the rotation rule as built from then on. The options not chosen were (b), keeping the build and recording the replaced `jti` where the cascade does not walk it, and (c), keeping the build as it was (`RotInherit`).
3. **CX-3 A playground bearer outlives the revocation of its subject (FM-1).** Settled by the owner on 2026-11-22, choosing option (a): the playground mint records the playground bearer in `issued_tokens` with the subject's `jti` as `parent_jti` in the `apiKey` mode, so the cascade revokes it and every replica refuses it through the revocation cache. It also closes gap G2. Change 0166 carries it. The options not chosen were (b), stating that revoking a subject leaves its playground bearers live until their `exp`, and (c), having the revocation request write the playground revocation marker.
4. **CX-4 An impersonation bearer outlives and escapes the caller's token (FM-2).** Settled by the owner on 2026-11-22, choosing the decision page's option (a) of FM-2: cap the bearer's `exp` at the caller token's `exp` as well as at `durationSeconds`, and record the caller token on the bearer's row so that revoking the caller's token ends the session. The page's option combines the clamp of option (a) above with the cascade of option (b) above; option (a) above alone would have left the session live after the caller's token is revoked. The page names the caller token as the bearer's parent, while rule 2 of §13.3 **Platform-admin impersonation.** identifies an impersonation row by `act_sub IS NOT NULL AND parent_jti IS NULL`, so change 0166, which carries the fix, settles whether the caller's `jti` goes in `parent_jti` with a new row predicate or in a separate column the cascade walks, as option (b) above states. The option not chosen was (c), keeping the rule and stating both behaviours.
5. **CX-5 An upstream ID token cannot be named as a revocation root (FM-3).** Settled by the owner on 2026-11-22, choosing option (a): a revocation request accepts a root without an `issued_tokens` row, walks `parent_jti` from the presented `jti`, and inserts a revoked row for the root, so the Token Service also refuses it as a subject or actor afterwards. It also closes gap G3. Change 0166 carries it. The options not chosen were (b), stating that an ID token is not a revocation root, and (c), recording a row for every ID token the Token Service accepts as a subject.
6. **CX-6 A child is broader than its actor (FM-4).** Settled by the owner on 2026-11-22, choosing option (a): a child mint requires the actor token's scope to grant every issued value as well as the subject's, so a child is no broader than either token. Change 0166 carries it. The option not chosen was (b), keeping the subject as the only bound on reach.
7. **CX-7 Strictly narrower by values admits a copy at equal reach (FM-5).** Settled by the owner on 2026-11-22, choosing option (b) of this decision, which the decision page listed as option (a) of FM-5: keep the comparison on scope values, and state that a domain wildcard is broader than a list of the domain's actions because it also admits tools added later. The rule does not change; change 0166 states it in §13.3. The options not chosen were (a), comparing reach under the tool catalog, and (c), refusing a request that names every action of a domain the subject holds by wildcard.
8. **CX-8 A child's depth can fall below its subject's (FM-6).** Settled by the owner on 2026-11-22, choosing option (a): rule (b) of §13.3 states that the `delegation_depth` comparison is with the actor for a child mint and with the subject otherwise. The code does not change; change 0166 carries the text. The option not chosen was (b), setting a child's depth to the larger of the two depths plus one.
9. **CX-9 A delegated token loses its `act` claim on a narrowing or a rotation (FM-7).** Settled by the owner on 2026-11-22, choosing option (a): every exchange without `actor_token` copies the subject's `act` claim onto the issued token. Change 0166 carries it. The option not chosen was (b), refusing an exchange without `actor_token` whose subject carries `act`.
10. **CX-10 A revoked caller token authenticates a mint (FM-8).** Settled by the owner on 2026-11-22, choosing option (a): every mint reads the caller token's `issued_tokens` row in step (1a) and refuses a revoked caller with `401 invalid_client`, as 0164 decision 9 does for the revocation request. It also closes gap G4. Change 0166 carries it. The option not chosen was (b), stating that a mint does not check the caller's revocation.
11. **CX-11 A full revoke misses the delegations the user made for another identity (FM-9).** Settled by the owner on 2026-11-22, choosing option (a): step 5 of the full revoke of a user (§11.4) revokes the lineage descendants of every token it revokes. Change 0166 carries it. The options not chosen were (b), also revoking the rows whose `act_sub` is the user, and (c), keeping step 5.
12. **CX-13 Whether any token of the user may send the revocation request (0164 O4, SM-13).** Settled by the owner on 2026-11-22, choosing option (a): keep decision 9 of 0164 as written, so any token that carries the user's identity may revoke that user's tokens. Change 0164 carries it as its decision 11, and §13.3 states the bound `O4_revocation_bounded_by_holding` checks. The options not chosen were (b), accepting the user's own caller only when its token is the subject token or one of its ancestors, and (c), accepting the user's own caller only when its token carries no `scope` claim or `tools:*`.
13. **Where the fixes for the model's findings land (FM-0, FM-10).** Settled by the owner on 2026-11-22, choosing option (a) of FM-0: change 0164 merged once SM-12 and SM-13 were answered, and a new change 0166 carries the fixes of decisions 3 to 11, with the model rerun against it. Under FM-10, option (a), 0166 also closes gap G1: the Token Service applies the default `audience` `lenny-gateway` that the §13.3 parameter table names when a request omits `audience`, so an exchanged token carries an `aud` claim and a self-exchange without `audience` is a rotation. The options not chosen were (b) of FM-0, adding the fixes to 0164 before it merged, and (b) of FM-10, stating that there is no default audience.
14. **Next steps for the model (FM-11).** Settled by the owner on 2026-11-22, choosing option (a): accept the base-scope results for `P7_depth_monotone_along_lineage` and `P9_lifetime_bounded_by_lineage_parent`, whose large-scope checks did not finish; change 0166 files gaps G5, G6, and G7 as issues; step 0165.S2 runs after 0166 lands; and the TLA+ model of the concurrent orderings stays a candidate until 0165.S2 is done. The option not chosen was (b), building the TLA+ model now.
