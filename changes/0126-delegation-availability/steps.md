## Steps

### 0126.S1 Delegation availability
- status: Planned
- aliases: [DA-A, DA-1, DA-2, DA-3, DA-4, DA-5, DA-6, DA-7]
- lanes: [specification, code, documentation]
- depends-on: [0119.S1, 0149.S3, 0149.S10, 0142.S2 (part)]
- closes: [F-8.4.4 (part)]

The step extends the calling-session parent and the allow-list that 0149.S3 (TS-3) adds, and the stored `maxDepth`, `DELEGATION_DISABLED`, and the list filter that 0149.S10 adds. It reads the runtime surface after 0119.S1 of [`0119-agent-runtimes-only`](../0119-agent-runtimes-only/steps.md#steps) removes `type: mcp`, so it carries no `allowDelegation` refusal for that type. Under decision 39 of [`0166-delegation-primitives`](../0166-delegation-primitives/decisions.md#decisions), the step is cut back to deciding whether a session holds a session grant. It carries the specification edits; the levels, the resolver, and the refusal with `disabledBy`; the rule that a session receives a grant at creation only when it may delegate (O18), which 0166.S1 reads when it creates the grant; the operations a session with children keeps (DAR-6); the `ask_question` list rule; the resolver that the `delegation` and `questions` values of "RP-4" read; the derived runtime rule and the tenant runtime entry; the tenant delegation settings route with `allowByDefault`, `policyRef`, and `ceilings` in the form O10 settles, whose `policyRef` and `ceilings` 0166.S1 reads; the derive narrowing; the explicit-zero per-hop switch and the extension rule; and the removal of the `approvalMode` argument and `DELEGATION_DENIED` with the proto reservation. The list rules of revisions 1 to 13 for the child tools are retired: the reference extension of 0166.S3 lists those tools from `availability.delegation`. 0166.S1 depends on this step. Until 0166.S1 replaces the gateway-hosted delegation tool with `session.create`, the refusal runs at that tool's handler, and 0166.S1 moves it to the handlers of the platform operations. Some parts read what other steps add, and the following records where each lands:

- The guardrail row of the resolution lands with or after 0142.S2 (the SO-2 proposal) of [`0142-security-oversight`](../0142-security-oversight/change.md#so-2-tenant-security-record-and-posture), which adds the record, hence the `(part)` entry. Whether the edge moves to a separate guardrail step of 0142 is O17 in `decisions.md`.
- The `ask_question` rule reads the session's `inputPolicy`, which R12 of [`0138-rest-only-client-api`](../0138-rest-only-client-api/change.md#ro-2-rest-additions) adds in 0138.S1. Revision 11 replaced the provider-surface rule of "MD-8", which 0135.S2 lands, with R12, and `lenny/request_input` leaves under E4, so the step no longer waits on 0135.S2 for either. 0138.S1 follows this step through 0135.S5, so an edge to it would close a cycle. Where the rule lands is O9 in `decisions.md`.
- The `capabilities.childSessions` condition: 0135.S5 of [`0135-message-delivery`](../0135-message-delivery/steps.md#steps) depends on this step for the capability, and this step's text placed the condition with or after 0135.S5. Which step adds the capability is O9. The tool names of "MD-23" leave the gateway under 0166, which names the operations, so this step carries no tool names. Whichever step lands the condition also adds `capabilities.childSessions: true` to the reference runtimes in `charts/lenny/templates/reference-runtimes.yaml` whose harness passes the validation DAR-7 names, and to the runtime fixtures of the delegation tests.
- The `delegation` and `questions` values in the availability computation of "RP-4", the `contract` cases for the `list_tools` frame and `TOOL_NOT_FOUND`, and the `e2e_kind` notice test land with 0139.S2 (RP-B) of [`0139-runtime-protocol`](../0139-runtime-protocol/steps.md#steps), which adds the frames and `UpdateAvailability` and depends on this step. This step supplies the resolver they read.
- The derive narrowing adds `delegationLease.maxDepth` to the derive route that 0144.S1 of [`0144-session-derive`](../0144-session-derive/change.md#sd-1-one-route) reshapes. Neither step depends on the other, and whichever lands second adds the field to that route.

The step closes the self-refusal half of F-8.4.4 by removing the `approvalMode` argument ("DA-6"). The other half, that no path stores `deny` on a lease, stays open, and O12 in `decisions.md` asks which change closes it.

Under decision 8 of [0117](../0117-gateway-package-layout/decisions.md), this step edits `spec/interfaces/gateway-seams.md` and `pkg/gateway/seam` for the gateway seam operations it changes. It adds one operation served by the session core to the platform tools seam, which returns "may delegate", `disabledBy`, and the effective settings of "DA-3" for a session. The handler of today's delegation tool and the delegation service call it for the refusal of "DA-3", and 0166.S1's operation handlers and grant creation reuse it: these are crossings from the platform tools seam to the session core that have no operation yet, because the seam may not import the tenant store. The resolver in `pkg/delegation` is a pure function the session core calls. 0139.S2, which depends on this step, reuses the operation for the availability of "RP-4" and adds none for it.
