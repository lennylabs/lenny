# Decisions: 0168 Token rule fixes from the property tests

## Open decisions

6. **The form of the start record (TOKX-I17).** Decision 5 records, in the administrator's own tenant, which bearers started an impersonation and in which target tenant, so that the impersonation step runs a transaction in a target tenant only when a token of the subtree started an impersonation there, and without `__all__`. TOKX-I17's suggested resolution offers two forms, and neither the issue nor 0167 decision 12 chooses one. Options:
   - (a) A tenant-scoped table, written by the first transaction of rule 1 (c), with one row per impersonation: the administrator's `tenant_id`, the caller bearer's `jti`, the target tenant, the impersonation bearer's `jti`, and its `exp`. `tenant_id` leads the primary key, the table carries a row-level security policy and the tenant guard trigger, and the expired-row GC and tenant deletion remove its rows.
   - (b) A column on the caller bearer's own `issued_tokens` row, such as an array of the target tenants, which rule 1 (c) appends to under a lock on that row.

   Recommendation: (a). A caller bearer without an `issued_tokens` row starts impersonations today (`checkCaller` in `pkg/credential/impersonation/service.go` passes it, and `impersonationRow` records its `jti` in the impersonator link), so under (b) its impersonations would have no record. Recording them would need either a row inserted for a token the Token Service did not issue, which step (1a), the full revoke's seed, and the expired-row GC would then read as an issued token, or a fallback that runs the step under `__all__` for a subtree with a token without a row, which keeps the exception decision 5 removes. A table also takes concurrent impersonation starts by the same bearer as independent inserts, and its rows expire with the impersonation bearer they name.

7. **Which invalidation levels and which tokens the token endpoint refuses (FM-16).** Decision 4 records the owner's answer as a refusal of "a `subject_token` or a caller token whose user is invalidated, reading the same invalidation status the gateway reads". Option (a) of the question on the decision page read "refuses a caller, a `subject_token`, or an `actor_token` whose user row records a full revoke or a hard disable" (open decision 17 of 0165 as raised in `cafb45e73`). The two texts differ on the levels and on the `actor_token`. The gateway's session-creation and interaction gate (`activeUserRefusal`, through `userstore.User.IsActive`) refuses every level of §11.4, and the rule 1 (c) fence of **Platform-admin impersonation.** (`checkUserNotInvalidated`) refuses hard disable and full revoke only. The words also cover the `subject_token` of a recursive-revocation request, which names the token to revoke and confers no authority. Options:
   - (a) Refuse a user whose row records a hard disable or a full revoke (`deleted_at` set, the read of rule 1 (c)), for the caller, the `subject_token`, and the `actor_token` of every mint, and for the caller of a recursive-revocation request. The `subject_token` of a recursive-revocation request is accepted.
   - (b) As (a), and refuse a soft-disabled user as well, as the gateway's session-creation gate does.
   - (c) As (a), and refuse the `subject_token` of a recursive-revocation request as well.

   Recommendation: (a). It is the option text the owner chose, and its levels are the ones the gateway's other token-side read uses. §11.4 gives a soft disable the effect "Deny new sessions" only, so refusing the soft-disabled user's mints would stop the delegations of the sessions the user already runs, while a hard disable also blocks new delegated tasks, which a child mint serves. Accepting the `subject_token` of a revocation request keeps a `tenant-admin` able to record a revoked root for an unrecorded token of the invalidated user, which removes access only, as **Recursive-revocation request authorization.** already accepts an already-revoked `subject_token` for the same reason.

## Decisions

The owner answered FM-12 to FM-16 on the M3 readiness decision page on 2026-11-23, each with option (a). The answers are recorded as decisions 15 to 17 of change 0165 (a formal model of the token rules) and decisions 12 and 13 of change 0167 (token rule fixes from the formal model), which name this change as the one that carries the fixes. The issues TOKX-I19, TOKX-I20, and TOKX-I22 are on the branch of 0165.S2 until it merges.

1. **F1: a recursive-revocation request accepts a caller that its own impersonation step revoked (0165 decision 15, FM-14 (a), TOKX-I22).** The cascade accepts a caller whose `jti` is in the `RETURNING` set of the same request's impersonation update, passed to `RevokeLineage`, and refuses every other revoked caller as now. Items 3 and 4 of **Token rule properties.** in §13.3 state the rule without an exception. The generator exclusion (`callerEndedByLink` in `tests/component/tokenproperties/generated_test.go`) and the skip of the subtest "an impersonation bearer as the caller of its root's revocation revokes the root" are removed. Owner's answer to FM-14, 2026-11-23. The options not chosen were (b), having the impersonation step leave the caller's own row live for the cascade to revoke after the caller check, and (c), keeping the rules with item 3 stating the outcome as an exception.
2. **TOKX-I20: the caller's share lock covers the impersonation step.** 0165 decision 15 assigns the fix of TOKX-I20 to this change together with F1, and TOKX-I20's suggested resolution states the mechanism this change uses: the caller's row is read under `FOR SHARE` in the transaction that revokes the impersonation rows, and a revoked caller is refused there. The other mechanism the issue names, one transaction under `__all__` for the subtree read and the update, conflicts with decision 5. Owner's answer to FM-14, 2026-11-23.
3. **F2: a full revoke starts its walk from the user's delegations (0165 decision 16, FM-15 (a), TOKX-I19).** The full revoke of a user also seeds its lineage walk with the child rows whose `act_sub` is the user and whose `parent_jti` is set, which are the delegations the user made with any token, recorded or not. The skip of the subtest "a full revoke reaches the delegations made with a token without a row" is removed. Owner's answer to FM-15, 2026-11-23. The options not chosen were (b), having step (1a) record a row for a `subject_token` or `actor_token` that has none, and (c), narrowing §11.4 step 5 and item 10 to the user's recorded tokens and their descendants.
4. **FM-16: the token endpoint refuses the tokens of an invalidated user (0165 decision 17, FM-16 (a)).** The token endpoint refuses a `subject_token` or a caller token whose user is invalidated, reading the same invalidation status the gateway reads. Open decision 7 settles which levels and which tokens. The refusal relates to [AUTHN-I1](../../issues/AUTHN/AUTHN-I1-no-way-to-lift-a-user-invalidation.md), because nothing lifts an invalidation today, and this change does not settle AUTHN-I1. No issue tracks FM-16, and this change files none, because it is the fix. Owner's answer to FM-16, 2026-11-23. The options not chosen were (b), stating the outcome in §13.3 as accepted, and (c), having the full revoke also remove the user's platform-managed role assignment.
5. **TOKX-I17: the impersonation step runs in a target tenant only when a start record names it, and without `__all__` (0167 decision 12, FM-12 (a)).** This change records, in the administrator's own tenant, which bearers started an impersonation and in which target tenant, and runs the impersonation step in a target tenant only when a token of the subtree started an impersonation there. It removes the exception that §4.2 and the pairing rule of R-02 in §12.3 name for callers without `platform-admin`, so only `platform-admin` paths set `__all__`. 0167 decision 12 kept the exception until this change and named, as the follow-up's mechanism, its option (b): a record in the impersonator's tenant of the target tenant and `jti` of each impersonation, and one tenant-scoped transaction per target tenant without `__all__`. Owner's answer to FM-12, 2026-11-23. Open decision 6 settles the form of the record. 0167 decision 13 (FM-13 (a)) leaves the other routes that revoke an administrator's bearer as they are.
