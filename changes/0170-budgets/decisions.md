# Decisions: 0170 Budgets

## Open decisions

None. The owner settled every open decision on 2026-11-23.

## Decisions

Settled in [`0166-delegation-primitives`](../0166-delegation-primitives/decisions.md#decisions) and binding on this change:

1. **The carve is a reservation from the parent's budget objects (0166's O4, its decision 12).** The owner chose option (a) on 2026-11-23: 0166's "DP-3" states a child session's carved budget as a reservation from the budget objects that apply to the parent, with the child's share as a budget object of the child's session funded by that reservation, and 0166.S2 waits on this change's step that adds budget objects. "BG-8" states the carve, and 0170.S1 is that step. Under 0166's decision 35 the carve's mechanics belong to this change, and 0166's "DP-3" cites "BG-8".
2. **This change joins the v1 program (O21).** The owner agreed on 2026-11-23, in the overlap review of 0166 revision 6 ("13. ok"), recorded as 0166's decision 43. 0170 is a member of [v1](../programs/v1.md), and 0166.S2 depends on 0170.S1.
3. **Budgets and billing are independent (owner's direction).** On 2026-11-23 the owner asked that what is measured stay consistent with billing "as much as possible", and then settled the relation: "Ideally we want the same numbers between billing and budgeting, but I don't think that's always possible or necessary. I'd rather keep them independent, with the operator making sure they align. Discounts, offers, special deals, etc. are complex and can make billing differ substantially from budgeting." Every meter of "BG-4" reads the records that 0121's billing events report, so budgets and billing count from the same sources, and Lenny promises no equality between a budget's figure and a bill. The billing stream gains nothing from this change: it already carries each pod's resources and time, from which a billing system can compute compute units by the same formula.
4. **Compute units follow a pod's resources (O1).** The owner chose option (a) on 2026-11-23 and then specified it: "COmpute units should already reflect the resource class. One minute on a small pod should not be equivalent to one minute on a large one ... Classes defined by tenants need to be handled too." A session's compute units per second are its pod's effective requests, vCPU times a CPU rate plus GiB of memory times a memory rate plus GPUs times a rate per GPU type, times a multiplier per isolation profile, divided by the pod's slot count. A `platform-admin` sets the rates and multipliers for the deployment, with defaults, and may pin a figure for one resource class or pool. Every class, including one a tenant defines, is priced from its resources, and a `tenant-admin` sets none of it. The operator prices a compute unit in money.
5. **Credits stay the unit of model calls (O2).** The owner chose option (a) on 2026-11-23: "(a)". A price list prices credits, compute units, and stored bytes separately.
6. **Who creates budgets, and the caps on their number (O4).** The owner chose option (a) on 2026-11-23, "Ok but caps should be configurable per deployment at least", and then chose option (c) of the follow-up on caps per tenant: "(c)". A `platform-admin` creates platform budgets and tenant budgets of any tenant, a `tenant-admin` creates tenant budgets of its own tenant, and a user or a service account creates principal budgets that only it attaches. The caps of "BG-1" bound the number of budgets:
   - `budgets.maxPerTenant` (default 500) caps the tenant budgets of one tenant, and `budgets.maxPerPrincipal` (default 20) caps the principal budgets of one principal. Each Helm value is the default for every tenant. A `platform-admin` may set either on one tenant's record, higher or lower, up to `budgets.maxPerTenantCeiling` (default 5000) and `budgets.maxPerPrincipalCeiling` (default 200), which bound what one tenant can make the shared stores hold.
   - A `tenant-admin` may lower the principal cap for its own tenant's principals, and never above the value in force for the tenant.
   - `budgets.maxRefsPerSession` (default 8) caps the references one session creation names. It is set for the deployment alone, because it bounds the reservations of every charge on the shared Redis.
7. **One operator price list, with tenant overrides of credit conversions alone (O3).** The owner agreed on 2026-11-23 ("yes, it does") to this form, after: "I agree price shouldn't be included in the cards, which should only be in credits. I agree there can be a per-upstream override for those that don't follow the standard conversion factor. Tenants could define their own conversions, including overriding platform ones" and "Can't chargeback be handled independently by the tenant?" Rate cards stay in credits. A `platform-admin` writes the price list: a standard money price per credit with per-upstream overrides, the price of a compute unit, and the price of storage. A `tenant-admin` may write credit conversions for any upstream the tenant uses, platform upstreams included, which apply to the tenant's own, principal, and session money budgets and never to a platform budget. Compute and storage count at the operator's prices. Chargeback and markups stay outside Lenny. Decision 38 limits a tenant's conversions to limits that the tenant, its principals, or a client wrote.
8. **Groups, environments, and principals bind a team's budget (O5).** The owner chose option (b) on 2026-11-23: "O5: (b)". A selector may name `groups`, the owner's directory groups at the session's creation under 0149.S9, beside `environments` and `principals`, which the client cannot choose freely. A budget that selects on `labels` binds only a root client that sets the label, and the documentation states it. An environment refuses no session for a missing label.
9. **0133.S3 builds no credit scopes (O6).** The owner chose option (a) on 2026-11-23: "O6: (a)". 0133.S3 builds rate cards, the per-call ceiling, the input estimate, settlement, the recovery sweep, and usage records, and 0170.S1 lands the budget set as the only set of budgets the proxy reserves against. 0133 records it as its decision 11, and its "Scopes" under "LU-6" names the budget set.
10. **Reservations move into the new period at a reset (O7).** The owner chose option (a) on 2026-11-23: "O7: (a)". Each reservation outstanding at a boundary, carves included, moves into the new period, and its settlement counts there. A charge that straddles a boundary counts wholly in the new period.
11. **No rollover (O8).** The owner chose option (b) on 2026-11-23: "O8: no rollover". A period starts at the budget's limit whatever the last period left unused, and a budget has no `rollover` field. The owner asked in the same answer whether the deployer can extend the budgeting primitives, and an assessment of that question is under way.
12. **Adjustments are an administrator's tool, for any current or future period (assessment of extensible budgets).** On 2026-11-23 the owner answered the assessment of extensible budgets: "keep period adjustments as an admin tool. An admin should be able to adjust any future period, not just the current one. For example, a user has daily limits and the admin wants to adjust up the user's limits for the next 3 days so that they can run tests", and "Drop the controller based approach." An adjustment raises or lowers a budget's limit for the periods that start in a window, for one partition or all ("BG-7"). Period policies such as rollover are not left to external controllers: Lenny ships one as a budget field if the owner asks for it later.
13. **An interceptor may attach budgets (assessment of extensible budgets).** The owner agreed on 2026-11-23: "Yes to the interceptor changes you described." A `PreRoute` interceptor may add references and lower the session budget, never remove a budget or raise one, and may name only a budget that permits it ("BG-3", **Budgets attached by an interceptor**).
14. **No meters defined by a deployer (assessment of extensible budgets).** The owner answered on 2026-11-23: "No to deployer-defined meters. Those should never live in Lenny. However, we should provide a way for a deployer to match external costs to sessions and therefore directory groups, environments, etc." A deployer attributes an external cost to a session with the identity assertion of 0124 "CR-2", the `Lenny-*` headers of 0133 "LU-11", or the client's own session identifier, and enforces its own limit with interceptors ("BG-3", **External costs**). Decision 15 states what the session read adds.
15. **The session read returns the groups recorded at creation (O22).** The owner chose option (a) on 2026-11-23: "O22: (a)". `GET /v1/sessions/{id}` returns, to a `tenant-admin` and a `platform-admin`, the directory groups recorded at the session's creation, and `GET /v1/sessions/{id}/budgets` its budget set. The identity assertion of 0124 and the `Lenny-*` headers of 0133 carry no groups or labels. Decision 40 replaces the administrators with the holders of "Read session attributes".
16. **`llmCredits` names the unit of model calls.** On 2026-11-23 the owner asked: "we should probably call it 'tokenCredits' or something like that. 'credits' is too generic now that budgeting covers different meters", and then chose `llmCredits`: "1: llmCredits". The unit `llmCredits` replaces `credits` wherever a budget names a unit. It matches 0133's `quotas.llmCredits` settings, and it covers the server tools that a rate card also prices in credits. 0133 keeps "credits" in its own prose about rate cards, and 0142's SO-5a condition `creditBudgetAbove` becomes `llmCreditsAbove`.
17. **Meters are separate from units.** The owner asked on 2026-11-23: "we shouldn't mix the "what" with the units used to measure it. For example, I might want to set a budget for tokens in currency, not credits. Same with all the others", and agreed to the proposal: "2: yes". A meter is what a charge measures, and a unit is what a limit counts it in. The meters are `llm`, `compute`, `storage`, `poolIdle`, `dedicatedNodes`, and `escrow`, and the units are `llmCredits`, `computeUnits`, `bytes`, `byteHours`, and `money`. "BG-4" states which units each meter allows: `llm` in `llmCredits` or `money`, `compute` and `poolIdle` in `computeUnits` or `money`, `storage` in `bytes`, `byteHours`, or `money`, `dedicatedNodes` in `money`, and `escrow` in `byteHours` or `money`.
18. **A budget holds several named limits.** The owner asked on 2026-11-23: "maybe a budget could contain more than one "what"? that would reduce the number of budget objects, in practice", and agreed to the proposal: "3: yes". A budget's `limits` map holds, under a key, `{meters, amount, unit}` with optional `thresholds` and `onExhausted`, and the limits share one selector, attachment, `usableBy`, `per`, period, mode, `notify`, and `startsAt` and `endsAt` ("BG-1"):
   - A limit may combine meters only in a unit that every one of them allows.
   - A charge must fit every limit that counts its meter, in every budget of its set.
   - The exhaustion of one limit stops the work of its meters alone.
   - A limit in `bytes` ignores the period.
   - `budgets.maxLimitsPerBudget` (default 8) caps the limits of one budget.
   - Every limit of a budget partition shares one Redis hash tag, so one script reserves them together.
   - A session budget has the same structure, and its defaults and ceilings join by key ("BG-2").
   - An adjustment names limits by key, `limits: {key: amount}`, each amount in its limit's unit ("BG-7"). This supersedes the `{amount, unit}` form of an adjustment recorded with decision 15.
19. **Actions at exhaustion (O9).** The owner chose option (a) on 2026-11-23: "O9: (a)". Each limit's `onExhausted.running` is `continue`, `release`, or `end`, with `release` the default for a limit that counts `compute` and `continue` otherwise, and a checkpoint is refused like any other write once a limit that counts `storage` is exhausted, with no margin. Decision 42 replaces `release` with `suspend`.
20. **Lease extension for session budgets and carves alone (O10).** The owner chose option (a) on 2026-11-23: "O10: (a)". §8.6 extends the limits of a session budget and of a carve. A refusal by a shared budget stands until an administrator raises or adjusts the limit, resets the budget, or its period ends.
21. **A session budget stops counting at the session's end (O12).** The owner chose option (a) on 2026-11-23: "O12: (a)". A session's stored data keeps counting against the shared budgets that match it for as long as it is held.
22. **Session budget defaults and ceilings match by limit key (O23).** The owner chose option (a) on 2026-11-23: "O23: (a)". A ceiling whose key the client did not use is added to the session budget, and a limit the client adds under a new key only narrows.
23. **A carve reserves where its unit can be expressed (O24).** The owner chose option (a) on 2026-11-23: "O24: (a)". A carve is reserved in each parent limit that counts the same meters in the same unit, and in each limit in money after conversion at the price list in force, and a parent limit in which it cannot be expressed takes the child's charges directly.
24. **Budgets are best effort: session budgets and carves reserve ahead, and shared budgets count afterwards (O11).** The owner chose option (d) on 2026-11-23: "Go with (d). Budgets will be best effort and not always strictly enforced." A session budget, and the carves that fund it, reserve each model call, compute tick, and write ahead in the session's own key, whose limits share one hash tag. A shared budget, principal, tenant, or platform, holds no reservation: each gateway replica admits work on its cached state of the budget, sums the settled charges in memory, and flushes the sums to Redis every `budgets.shared.flushSeconds` (default 5, from 1 to 60), refreshing its cached state at each flush, and a recovery sweep adds a lapsed replica's unflushed charges from the durable records of each meter after the replica's flush watermark. A shared budget never switches to per-call reservation near its amount, which was option (c). Its overshoot is about one flush interval of spending across replicas plus the work in flight when its cached state turns to exhausted ("BG-6", **Best effort**; BGR-9). A carve is reserved in the parent's session budget alone, so decision 23 applies to the limits of session budgets, and shared budgets count a child's charges as they count any session's ("BG-8"). No reservation crosses a reset, and decision 10 holds as the rule that a charge counts in the period in which it settles. No reservation crosses hash slots, and no budget hands out allotments, which settles open decision 6 of [`0133-llm-upstreams-and-cost-budgets`](../0133-llm-upstreams-and-cost-budgets/decisions.md#decisions), recorded there as its decision 6, the number it had while open. Decisions 35 and 36 bound a shared budget during a Redis outage and a burst of writes to a shared storage limit.
25. **The checkpoint that `release` takes passes an exhausted storage limit.** The owner wrote on 2026-11-23, with decision 24: "For this same reason, maybe we want to allow checkpoints on release even if that means going over the storage limit (to your previous question)". This amends decision 19 for that one checkpoint: the checkpoint that `release` takes when a session becomes `dormant` under `onExhausted.running` is admitted past an exhausted limit that counts `storage`, and every other checkpoint and write is still refused. The overshoot is one checkpoint of the session's workspace, which the size limits of the pod's volumes bound, and no setting is added ("BG-6", **Exhaustion**). O25 asks whether a session that becomes `dormant` for another reason takes the same exception.
26. **Budget notices go to the audit log and the operational event stream (O14).** The owner chose option (b) on 2026-11-23: "O14: (b)", in the form revised after decision 3 and the assessment of extensible budgets. A threshold crossing, an exhaustion, and a period's close are audit events and events on the operational event stream of §25.5, whose subscriptions carry a tenant filter. A session receives a `budget_notice` event when its own charge crosses a threshold of its session budget. No billing event carries a notice, and a budget has no `notify` field ("BG-6", **Notices**). This amends decision 18, which listed `notify` among the fields a budget's limits share. Decision 45 sends `budget_notice` to the client alone.
27. **No money on billing events (O15).** The owner chose option (a) on 2026-11-23: "O15: (a)". Billing events carry quantities and 0133's estimated credits, and money appears in budget usage reports and budget events alone, labelled as an estimate.
28. **Every checkpoint that makes a session `dormant` passes an exhausted storage limit (O25).** The owner chose option (a) on 2026-11-23: "O25: (a) yes". The exception of decision 25 covers the checkpoint that SL-6 of 0146 takes when a session's idle timer fires, with the same overshoot of one checkpoint per transition.
29. **Costs of a tenant with no session feed no budget (O13).** The owner asked on 2026-11-23 how a budget that counts them would stop them, "Budgets are only used to reject new sessions or stop existing ones. They don't scale down any resources", and chose option (b): "never count them. The operator/deployer/tenant can monitor spend with real pricing (discounts, etc.) outside of lenny using the billing events". The idle pods of a tenant's owned or pinned pools, its dedicated nodes, and legal-hold escrow stay billing events of 0121, and the meters `poolIdle`, `dedicatedNodes`, and `escrow` leave the design. 0170.S5 is cancelled.
30. **Today's quota fields become budgets (O16).** The owner chose option (a) on 2026-11-23: "ok", after decision 29 settled that only usage a refusal can stop feeds a budget. The tenant's `storageQuotaBytes`, `TokenQuotaPerWindow`, and `QuotaResetPeriod`, and 0133's `quotas.llmCredits.perUser`, `perTenant`, and `global`, leave. Budgets that `budgets.tenantDefaults` instantiates and platform budgets take their place, and `STORAGE_QUOTA_EXCEEDED` retires into `BUDGET_EXHAUSTED`. `quotas.llmCredits.perCall` stays, and concurrency and rate limits stay quotas under §11.1.
31. **A runtime cannot read its budgets (O17).** The owner chose option (c) on 2026-11-23: "(c) nothing. There's no guarantee session budgets will exist (they should be optional) so the runtime may not always get the budget info, making this mechanism inconsistent and unreliable. We could fake a large enough session budget, I guess, but I'm not sure it's worth it?" A runtime learns of budgets through refusals alone, and Lenny presents no stand-in session budget to a session without one. A carve that names no amount takes `defaultDelegationFraction` of the parent's session budget, or none when the parent has none ("BG-8").
32. **Partitions (O18).** The owner chose option (a) on 2026-11-23: "O18: ok". `per` keeps one set of counters per value of the named attribute, each limit at its full amount.
33. **No egress meter (O19).** The owner chose option (a) on 2026-11-23: "O19: (a)". A deployer that pays for egress through its own proxy attributes it with the identity of decision 14, and a later change adds egress counters to 0127 if egress budgets are wanted.
34. **One currency per deployment, which may be invented (O20).** The owner chose option (a) on 2026-11-23: "O20: (a) but currency could be an invented currency too". `budgets.currency` takes an ISO 4217 code or a code the operator invents, and Lenny converts between no currencies.

Settled on 2026-11-23 from the review of 0166 and 0170 with the branch's edits to the other v1 changes, recorded in `review-history.md` as R1 to R13. Each quotes the owner's answer to the finding as presented.

35. **Shared budgets keep a bound when Redis or a flush fails (R1).** The owner answered "R1: ok". While a replica cannot reach Redis or its flushes fail, it admits work in each shared limit up to its fail-open share, what the limit had left at its last successful flush divided by the replicas it last saw, at most §12.4's per-replica hard cap, and `quotaFailOpenCumulativeMaxSeconds` turns it to refusing ("BG-6", **Fail-open**). BGR-9 states the term.
36. **A write to a shared storage limit checks its declared size (R2).** The owner chose option (b) on 2026-11-23: "R2: (b)", after: "I wouldn't assume writes are infrequent and large. We may want to think of some debouncing mechanism for these calls? Or maybe the call should check whether X bytes are available? Or something like that. I'm ok if we overshoot a bit. Also, remember that checkpoints when the pod is put to sleep should be stored no matter what. This is all blob storage we're talking about, right?" A replica admits a write when its declared size fits the replica's share of what the limit had left at its last flush, less what it admitted since, with no Redis call per write and the stream cut past its declared size ("BG-6"). Storage budgets count the object store, and a pod's disk and Postgres are outside them. This amends decision 24 for limits that count `storage` in `bytes`.
37. **No new or resumed session while a storage limit is exhausted (R3).** The owner answered "R3: ok". Admission refuses a creation, a resume, and a child while a limit that counts `storage` in the session's set is exhausted, so the checkpoint admitted at a pod's release only serves sessions already live ("BG-6", **Admission**).
38. **A limit converts at the price list of the level that wrote it (R4).** The owner answered "R4: ok". A tenant's credit conversions apply to limits written by the tenant, its principals, or a client, and every limit a `platform-admin` wrote converts at the platform price list ("BG-5"). This amends decision 7.
39. **Delegation needs a session budget by default (R5).** The owner answered "R5: I would say (c), which is to refuse delegation if no session budget has been set. Or maybe (c) and (b) and the behavior is selected via config at different levels", and then "ok" to the proposal. `gateway.delegation.sessionBudget` takes `requiredToDelegate`, the default, `requiredToCreate`, or `optional`, at the platform, the tenant, and the environment, a lower level only stricter. 0166 owns the setting and its refusal as its decision 52 ("DP-1"), and "BG-2" and "BG-8" cite it.
40. **"Read session attributes" (R6).** The owner answered "R6: ok". A permission that returns a session's recorded directory groups, its labels, and its budget set and nothing else, grantable to `billing-viewer` or a custom role, so a deployer's cost service needs no administrator's credential ("BG-10"). This amends decision 15.
41. **Label rules (R7).** The owner answered "R7: I would restrict who can use it. How would that work?", then "label rule. Labels without a rule can be used freely. Make rules flexible enough (run a proposal by me before making any edits)", and "R7: ok" to the proposal. 0149 owns the label rules as "Label rules (TS-10)", with its decision recording them, and "BG-3" reads them. A budget that selects on a key no rule governs is accepted with a `Warning`.
42. **A budget suspends a subtree, holding its pods (R8).** The owner answered "R8: budget wins. But in most cases the children would also hit the budget, right?", then "hmm maybe hitting the session budget should either put every session in the tree to sleep or none? Putting just some is not a very good behavior IMO", then "if the parent/client can adjust limits up in some cases, wouldn't moving the session subtree to `dormant` just tp have to wake it up again be a waste? Is there an existing state or new it could be moved to? Or could moving the session to dormant be applied only after a period of time?", and "ok" to the proposal. `onExhausted.running` takes `continue`, `suspend`, or `end`, and `suspend`, the default for a limit that counts `compute`, suspends the session with `suspension_cause: budget` and blocks its subtree under 0146 "SL-4", holds the pods for `budgets.suspendPodHoldSeconds`, resumes the subtree when the limit gains room, and checkpoints and releases the pods after the hold ("BG-6", **Budget suspension**). This amends decision 19, and decisions 25 and 28 cover the checkpoint at the end of the hold. 0146 records the state and its transitions.
43. **Budget keys carry no TTL (R9).** The owner answered "R9: ok". A session budget's keys live and end with the session, and `delegation.budgetKeyTTLSeconds`, §8.3's TTL re-arming, and `BUDGET_KEYS_EXPIRED` retire ("BG-9"). 0166 "DP-6" lists them, and 0146 "SL-10" cites this.
44. **`UPLOAD_TOO_LARGE` for 0147's upload cap (R10).** The owner answered "R10: ok" to a finding that presented `maxAssetUploadBytes` of 0147 as a cap on one upload. 0147's cap answers `413 UPLOAD_TOO_LARGE`, because it is no budget, and 0147 states it. The cap bounds a tenant's stored asset uploads, so 0147 raised its open decision 57, which the owner settled on 2026-11-23 as option (b): "go with (b)". The cap answers `429 ASSET_UPLOAD_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`, and `413 UPLOAD_TOO_LARGE` stays for an upload larger than the cap by itself.
45. **`budget_notice` goes to the client alone (R11).** The owner answered "R11: ok". The session's client receives `budget_notice` on the session event stream, and the runtime receives none, under decision 31 ("BG-6", **Notices**).
46. **SO-5a's credit condition covers extensions and every `llm` limit (R12).** The owner answered "R12: ok". `when.llmCreditsAbove` holds a granted extension of a carve, reads every limit that counts `llm`, converted when it is in money, and reads a child's resolved session budget when no carve funds it. 0142 "SO-5a" states the rule, and "BG-6" and "BG-8" cite it.
47. **No budget that was never reserved (R13).** The owner answered "R13: ok. Can you remind me what a carve is? Provide context". A transfer is refused with `BUDGET_EXHAUSTED` when the root cannot reserve the carve's unconsumed amount, a `PATCH` of a carved session budget is refused with `409 SESSION_BUDGET_CARVED`, and a `session.set_limits` raise with no carve behind it is refused with `409 SESSION_BUDGET_NOT_CARVED` ("BG-2", "BG-8").
