# Decisions: 0168 Token rule fixes from the property tests

## Open decisions

None. The owner settled open decisions 6 and 7 on 2026-11-23 (decisions 6 and 7). The answers to change 0169's counterexamples are decisions 8 to 12.

## Decisions

The owner answered FM-12 to FM-16 on the M3 readiness decision page on 2026-11-23, each with option (a). The answers are recorded as decisions 15 to 17 of change 0165 (a formal model of the token rules) and decisions 12 and 13 of change 0167 (token rule fixes from the formal model), which name this change as the one that carries the fixes. The issues TOKX-I19, TOKX-I20, and TOKX-I22 are on the branch of 0165.S2 until it merges.

1. **F1: a recursive-revocation request accepts a caller that its own impersonation step revoked (0165 decision 15, FM-14 (a), TOKX-I22).** The cascade accepts a caller whose `jti` is in the `RETURNING` set of the same request's impersonation update, passed to `RevokeLineage`, and refuses every other revoked caller as now. Items 3 and 4 of **Token rule properties.** in §13.3 state the rule without an exception. The generator exclusion (`callerEndedByLink` in `tests/component/tokenproperties/generated_test.go`) and the skip of the subtest "an impersonation bearer as the caller of its root's revocation revokes the root" are removed. Owner's answer to FM-14, 2026-11-23. The options not chosen were (b), having the impersonation step leave the caller's own row live for the cascade to revoke after the caller check, and (c), keeping the rules with item 3 stating the outcome as an exception.
2. **TOKX-I20: the caller's share lock covers the impersonation step.** 0165 decision 15 assigns the fix of TOKX-I20 to this change together with F1, and TOKX-I20's suggested resolution states the mechanism this change uses: the caller's row is read under `FOR SHARE` in the transaction that revokes the impersonation rows, and a revoked caller is refused there. The other mechanism the issue names, one transaction under `__all__` for the subtree read and the update, conflicts with decision 5. Owner's answer to FM-14, 2026-11-23.
3. **F2: a full revoke starts its walk from the user's delegations (0165 decision 16, FM-15 (a), TOKX-I19).** The full revoke of a user also seeds its lineage walk with the child rows whose `act_sub` is the user and whose `parent_jti` is set, which are the delegations the user made with any token, recorded or not. The skip of the subtest "a full revoke reaches the delegations made with a token without a row" is removed. Owner's answer to FM-15, 2026-11-23. The options not chosen were (b), having step (1a) record a row for a `subject_token` or `actor_token` that has none, and (c), narrowing §11.4 step 5 and item 10 to the user's recorded tokens and their descendants.
4. **FM-16: the token endpoint refuses the tokens of an invalidated user (0165 decision 17, FM-16 (a)).** The token endpoint refuses a `subject_token` or a caller token whose user is invalidated, reading the same invalidation status the gateway reads. Decision 7 settles which levels and which tokens. The refusal relates to [AUTHN-I1](../../../issues/AUTHN/AUTHN-I1-no-way-to-lift-a-user-invalidation.md), because nothing lifts an invalidation today, and this change does not settle AUTHN-I1. No issue tracks FM-16, and this change files none, because it is the fix. Owner's answer to FM-16, 2026-11-23. The options not chosen were (b), stating the outcome in §13.3 as accepted, and (c), having the full revoke also remove the user's platform-managed role assignment.
5. **TOKX-I17: the impersonation step runs in a target tenant only when a start record names it, and without `__all__` (0167 decision 12, FM-12 (a)).** This change records, in the administrator's own tenant, which bearers started an impersonation and in which target tenant, and runs the impersonation step in a target tenant only when a token of the subtree started an impersonation there. It removes the exception that §4.2 and the pairing rule of R-02 in §12.3 name for callers without `platform-admin`, so only `platform-admin` paths set `__all__`. 0167 decision 12 kept the exception until this change and named, as the follow-up's mechanism, its option (b): a record in the impersonator's tenant of the target tenant and `jti` of each impersonation, and one tenant-scoped transaction per target tenant without `__all__`. Owner's answer to FM-12, 2026-11-23. Decision 6 settles the form of the record. 0167 decision 13 (FM-13 (a)) leaves the other routes that revoke an administrator's bearer as they are.
6. **The start record is a tenant-scoped table (TOKX-I17).** The first transaction of rule 1 (c) of **Platform-admin impersonation.** writes one row per impersonation in the administrator's own tenant: the administrator's `tenant_id`, the caller bearer's `jti`, the target tenant, the impersonation bearer's `jti`, and its `exp`. `tenant_id` leads the primary key, the table carries a row-level security policy and the tenant guard trigger, and the expired-row GC and tenant deletion remove its rows. A table records the impersonations of a caller bearer without an `issued_tokens` row, which `checkCaller` in `pkg/credential/impersonation/service.go` accepts today, without inserting a row for a token the Token Service did not issue and without a fallback to `__all__`. Owner's answer to FM-17 on the M3 readiness decision page, 2026-11-23. The option not chosen was (b), a column of target tenants on the caller bearer's own `issued_tokens` row.
7. **The token endpoint refuses hard-disabled and fully revoked users (FM-16).** The token endpoint refuses a token whose user row records a hard disable or a full revoke (`deleted_at` set, the read of rule 1 (c)): the caller, the `subject_token`, and the `actor_token` of every mint, and the caller of a recursive-revocation request. The `subject_token` of a recursive-revocation request is accepted, because it names the token to revoke and confers no authority. A soft disable keeps its §11.4 effect, which denies new sessions only. Owner's answer to FM-18 on the M3 readiness decision page, 2026-11-23. The options not chosen were (b), also refusing a soft-disabled user, and (c), also refusing the `subject_token` of a recursive-revocation request.

Change 0169 (a concurrency model of the token rules) checked the locking design of decisions 1, 2, 5, and 6 with a TLA+ model before the code lane of 0168.S1, as its decision 2 states. The owner answered its counterexamples on 2026-11-23 as FM-26 to FM-29 on the M3 readiness decision page, and settled the bound of TOKX-I10 in chat on the same day. The answers are recorded as [decisions 10 to 13 of change 0169](../2026-11-23-0169-token-concurrency-model/decisions.md), which name this change as the one that carries them.

8. **R5: the impersonation step's target-tenant updates run in the outer transaction (0169 decision 10, FM-26 (a)).** The step revokes the impersonation rows of each other target tenant in the outer transaction rather than in a nested transaction, setting the target tenant's context with `SET LOCAL app.current_tenant` before each update and the request's tenant after it, and retries the step on `40P01` as `RevokeLineage` retries the cascade. Every wait between two such requests is then a lock wait that the deadlock detector resolves, and the aborted request's retry finds its caller revoked by the other request and is refused. TF-2 and the text of §4.2 change with it. Owner's answer to FM-26, 2026-11-23. The options not chosen were (b), keeping the nested transactions with a `lock_timeout` on each, and (c), keeping them and stating the wait in rule 7.
9. **TOKX-I13: the cascade and the full revoke take the per-tenant audit advisory lock (0169 decision 11, FM-27 (a)).** `revokeLineageOnce` and `RevokeBySubject` take the per-tenant audit advisory lock of §11.7 before the statement that reads their snapshot, as the `I13Lock` variant of 0169's model does, which held C1 and C7 in every configuration. The option placed the fix in this change or in a change of its own, and the owner left the packaging open. The orchestrator of the 2026-11-23 session placed it in this change, because the code lane of 0168.S1 has not started and the model checked `I13Lock` on top of this change's design. The owner decided the bound of TOKX-I10 with it (decision 12). Owner's answer to FM-27, 2026-11-23. The options not chosen were (b), keeping the window and stating it in §13.3 and §11.4, and (c), extending TF-4's user-row read to the playground mint.
10. **R6: a bearer row without a start record is accepted and stated (0169 decision 12, FM-28 (a)).** Rule 1 (c) states that a failed first transaction can leave a bearer row that no client holds, that the impersonation step does not reach, and that the `GET /v1/admin/impersonation` listing shows until it ends. Owner's answer to FM-28, 2026-11-23. The options not chosen were (b), committing the start record before the bearer row in a transaction of its own, and (c), ending the bearer row with `EndImpersonation` on such a failure.
11. **G3: the impersonation start reads the caller bearer's row under a share lock (0169 decision 13, FM-29 (b)).** The first transaction of rule 1 (c) reads the caller bearer's `issued_tokens` row under a share lock, which conflicts with the cascade's update of that row, so an impersonation either commits before the cascade's update reaches the bearer or reads it revoked, and rule 7's window ends when the cascade commits. This change reruns the model's `impersonation` and `nested_start` configurations, as "Validation owed" in `steps.md` states. Owner's answer to FM-29, 2026-11-23. The option not chosen was (a), stating in rule 7 that the window includes an impersonation whose caller check precedes the cascade's commit and whose record commits after it.
12. **TOKX-I10: a depth cap and a statement timeout (0169 decision 11).** An exchange whose subject token is already 32 links deep is refused, which needs a depth column on `issued_tokens` set at issuance to the parent's depth plus one. The cascade and the full revoke's walk run under a 5-second statement timeout, matching the 5-second lock acquisition timeout of §11.7, and a cascade that reaches it revokes nothing and answers `503` with a retryable error. Both values are operator-tunable defaults. Owner's answer in chat, 2026-11-23, asked because decision 9 ties TOKX-I10 to the advisory lock. The options not chosen were a statement timeout only and a depth cap only. TF-8 reads "the subject token" as the token the issued row records as its parent, which is the `actor_token` for `a2a_delegation` child minting, because a cap on the `subject_token` alone leaves a chain of child mints unbounded.
13. **FM-31: a refused impersonation start keeps its started event.** The first transaction of rule 1 (c) writes `admin.impersonation_started` before it reads the caller bearer's row under the share lock of decision 11, so a caller bearer that is revoked or is itself an impersonation bearer is refused after the started event is written, and the audit log holds a started event with no ended event for that attempt. No unlocked check runs before the started event. Owner's answer to FM-31 on the M3 readiness decision page, 2026-11-24. The option not chosen was (b), keeping an unlocked check before the started event so that most refusals write no audit row.
14. **FM-32: the cascade timeout uses the Lenny error envelope.** A cascade that reaches the timeout of decision 12 answers `503 REVOCATION_TIMEOUT`, category `TRANSIENT`, with `retryable: true` and `Retry-After`, in the Lenny error envelope that `KMS_SIGNING_UNAVAILABLE` uses on the same endpoint, and §15.1 lists the code. Owner's answer to FM-32 on the M3 readiness decision page, 2026-11-24. The option not chosen was (b), the OAuth error envelope with the error code `revocation_timeout`, as `token_store_unavailable` uses, which has no `retryable` field.
