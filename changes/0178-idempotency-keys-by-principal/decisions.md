# Decisions: 0178 Idempotency keys scoped by principal

## Open decisions

- **O2. Bearers of one subject that another party holds.** Raised by the security review of steps 0178.S1 and 0178.S2, 2026-11-26. The scope is the tenant, the subject, and the bearer's `session_id` claim. Three bearers carry the user's subject with no `session_id` claim and are held by another party or carry other authority: a token-exchange child whose `act` claim names a delegate (`pkg/tokenexchange/tokenexchange.go`), a platform-admin impersonation bearer (`pkg/credential/impersonation/service.go`, subject the target user), and a playground session-capability bearer (`pkg/gateway/playground/token.go`, no `session_id` claim). Each files under the user's own scope, so its holder who knows the user's key and body replays the user's stored response, including the upload token the 0173 replay restores, and the user replays theirs. "Limits, and alternatives rejected" in `change.md` already states that two bearers of one subject bound to no session share records. Options: (a) keep the scope and the stated limit, and say in §11.5 that a delegated, impersonating, or playground bearer of a user shares that user's records; (b) add the actor (`act.sub` and `act.session_id`, empty when absent) to the scope, a further primary-key column; (c) file an impersonation bearer under its own marker and a playground bearer under its origin. Recommendation: (b), because it closes the cross-party replay with one column that the resolver already has (`authmw.Principal.Act`) and keeps an ordinary user's scope unchanged. The step does not wait on it.
- **O3. Change 0138 R5 and the bound session.** Raised by the general review of steps 0178.S1 and 0178.S2, 2026-11-26. R5 of [0138](../0138-rest-only-client-api/change.md) states the full scope of an idempotency key as the tenant, the subject, the audience, `azp` or `client_id`, the method, the route template, the path parameters, and the key, and its step 0138.S1 says 0178.S1 scopes by the tenant and the subject. Decision 3 added the bound session, and migration 0205 keys by `(tenant_id, subject, session_id, idempotency_key)`, so 0138.S1 as written drops `session_id`. Options: (a) revise 0138 R5 and 0138.S1 to keep the bound session in the scope; (b) let 0138 replace the session dimension with its own. Recommendation: (a), so decision 3 survives 0138.

## Decisions

1. **Idempotency keys are scoped by principal (IK-3).** Idempotency records are scoped by the principal, the tenant together with the user (subject), through a change that edits §11.5 and the store. Owner's answer to IK-3 on the M3 readiness decision page, 2026-11-25, option (a). The option not chosen was (b), keeping the per-tenant scope and documenting it. The question arose from the review of change [0173](../archive/2026-11-25-0173-idempotency-stored-credentials/change.md), whose replay rebuilds an upload token for any caller that holds the key.
2. **User erasure deletes the user's idempotency records (ERASE-I2).** Deleting a user's idempotency records during user erasure, which revision 1 left out of scope, is added to this change as its next step, 0178.S2. Owner's direction in chat, 2026-11-25, with the words "Add the fix for the deletion of idempotency records to the list of next steps." The deletion finds the records by the subject that step 0178.S1 adds.
3. **The scope of a bearer bound to a session (O1).** When the principal carries a `SessionID`, as a session-capability bearer and the bearer of a delegated child do, the record scope includes it, so the scope is the tenant, the subject, and the session for such a bearer, and the tenant and the subject otherwise. The session id is an extra column of the primary key of `idempotency_keys`, `session_id`, which holds the empty string for an ordinary user token, because a primary-key column cannot be NULL. The option not chosen was (b), keeping the scope at the tenant and the subject for every principal, as decision 1 states it. Owner's answer to IP-1 (open decision O1) on the M3 readiness decision page, 2026-11-26, option (a).
