# Decisions: 0178 Idempotency keys scoped by principal

## Open decisions

None.

## Decisions

1. **Idempotency keys are scoped by principal (IK-3).** Idempotency records are scoped by the principal, the tenant together with the user (subject), through a change that edits §11.5 and the store. Owner's answer to IK-3 on the M3 readiness decision page, 2026-11-25, option (a). The option not chosen was (b), keeping the per-tenant scope and documenting it. The question arose from the review of change [0173](../archive/2026-11-25-0173-idempotency-stored-credentials/change.md), whose replay rebuilds an upload token for any caller that holds the key.
2. **User erasure deletes the user's idempotency records (ERASE-I2).** Deleting a user's idempotency records during user erasure, which revision 1 left out of scope, is added to this change as its next step, 0178.S2. Owner's direction in chat, 2026-11-25, with the words "Add the fix for the deletion of idempotency records to the list of next steps." The deletion finds the records by the subject that step 0178.S1 adds.
3. **The scope of a bearer bound to a session (O1).** When the principal carries a `SessionID`, as a session-capability bearer and the bearer of a delegated child do, the record scope includes it, so the scope is the tenant, the subject, and the session for such a bearer, and the tenant and the subject otherwise. The session id is an extra column of the primary key of `idempotency_keys`, `session_id`, which holds the empty string for an ordinary user token, because a primary-key column cannot be NULL. The option not chosen was (b), keeping the scope at the tenant and the subject for every principal, as decision 1 states it. Owner's answer to IP-1 (open decision O1) on the M3 readiness decision page, 2026-11-26, option (a). Decision 8 replaced the session dimension with the rotation chain on 2026-11-26. Restored by decision 12 on 2026-11-27 (owner's answer to UC-1 of change 0182): the session dimension is filled from the calling session of a pod-path call and from any other bearer's session id, as IP-2 of `change.md` states.

An ordinary user's scope keeps empty actor values. Step 0178.S3 builds it, and whether the platform-admin impersonation bearer and the playground session-capability bearer carry an `act` claim, or need their own marker to be scoped apart, is settled while building that step against the tree. Owner's answer to IP-2 in chat, 2026-11-26, option (a) on the M3 readiness decision page (option (b) above), with the words "IP-2: (a)". The options not chosen were keeping the scope and stating the limit in §11.5, and filing an impersonation bearer under its own marker and a playground bearer under its origin. Decision 8 replaced the acting party with the rotation chain on 2026-11-26. Narrowed by decision 13 on 2026-11-27 (owner's answer to IP-14): the intent holds through the three properties decision 13 names, and the acting-party columns are not rebuilt.

8. **The idempotency scope by rotation chain (O8, which settles O4, O5, and O6).** Option (a) of O8, with the changes an adversarial review found it needs. The owner asked on 2026-11-26 for an adversarial review of the recommendation and said "If sound, go with that"; the review found it sound with specific changes, which this decision applies. It revises decisions 3, 5, and 6.
   - **The value.** A rotation chain, a claim of its own on every Lenny JWT (`rch`) defined under its own bold label in §13.3 and distinct from the `parent_jti` lineage of **Lineage of derived tokens.**: a child, a narrowing, an impersonation, and the admin bootstrap mint each start a new chain, though a child and a narrowing remain lineage descendants. Only a self-rotation copies the subject's chain, decided before signing so that the claim, the audit `exchange_type`, and the revocation follow one decision; the bootstrap credential rotation copies the chain.
   - **Every input has a value.** A Lenny-signed token's chain is its claim, or its `jti` when the claim is absent. A keyed request whose Lenny token has neither is refused with `401` and the reason `principal_chain_required`, and nothing is filed under an empty value. The service identity and the dev-header path have defined values or refuse an idempotency key.
   - **The source is tagged by code path.** `authmw.Principal` gains a source set by the verifier that accepted the token (`lenny`, `idp`, `agent`, `service`, `dev`); `MultiVerifier` reports the member; nothing infers the source from `JTI`, `CallerType`, or `SessionID`.
   - **IdP tokens.** `normalizeUserBearer` clears the chain claim and `act`, which closes AUTHN-I2. An IdP token's chain is its `iss` with its `azp`, else its `client_id` (RFC 9068), else its sorted `aud`; all IdP tokens of one subject that name the same client, or name none and carry the same audiences, share a scope, and §11.5 states that limit.
   - **Playground.** In `oidc` mode the chain is a random value generated with the playground session record and stored in it, never the cookie value. In `apiKey` mode it derives from a digest of the pasted subject token's chain, namespaced `pg:`. In `dev` mode each mint starts a new chain, which §27 states.
   - **The scope.** The tenant, the subject, the chain with its source, the method, the mux route pattern with all of its wildcards (from `mux.Handler(r)`, not the allowed-paths entry), the path parameters, the effective audience of 0138 R5, and the key. An MCP tool call maps to `POST` and `mcp:<tool>`. The store keys rows by `tenant_id`, `subject`, and a SHA-256 digest of the canonical scope tuple, which keeps the key bounded. One migration replaces migration 0207 and empties the table.
   - **The retry cost, stated in §11.5 and `docs/client-guide/authentication.md`.** A key replays only for a bearer of the same chain. A holder of a Lenny-issued token keeps replay across a refresh only by self-rotation with the subject's full `audience`, and the rotation example passes it; a re-narrowing, a new mint, or a retry after `exp` executes again, and a pending original does not block it. IdP-direct clients and pod-path calls keep replay across refresh.
   - **Replay admission, a closed list.** Before a replay, the creating-caller check runs on the routes and MCP tools that create sessions (`lenny/create_session`, `lenny/delegate_task`), the §11.4 gate and the Article 18 restriction run where the handler runs them, and R5's operation check runs for a narrowed request. Resource-state, quota, and body-dependent capability checks are not rerun. A refused replay is not stored and leaves the record in place, and a creating-caller refusal at replay writes an audit row with its own `exchange_type`. The checks run as a per-route admit hook keyed by the mux pattern, and in the MCP hook.
   - **Refusals that can clear are not stored.** The gate's `403` refusals, `OPERATION_NOT_PERMITTED_FOR_RESOURCE`, and `429 QUOTA_EXCEEDED` are not stored, so a retry after the condition clears runs again ([APICONV-I8](../../issues/APICONV/APICONV-I8-idempotency-stores-refusals-that-can-clear.md)).
   - **Overlaps.** The records state how a pod-path `session.create` keyed under 0166 DP-1 relates to §11.5 records, and what 0138's removal of the MCP `idempotencyKey` means for pod-path platform tools.
   - **What closes into it.** O4 (impersonation and playground bearers), O6 (siblings and a self-relabelled child), and APICONV-I3, APICONV-I5, APICONV-I6, APICONV-I7, and AUTHN-I2. O5 has no subject left, because the scope no longer names the acting party. Decision 3's bound session, decision 5's acting party, and the scope lists of 0138 R5 under decision 6 give way to the chain; 0138 R5 keeps its effective audience, and `client_id` enters through the IdP chain. Step 0178.S3 is reworked on its branch, and step 0178.S4 builds the replay admission and the refusals that are not stored.
   - The option not chosen for now was the simpler alternative the review put forward: dedupe by subject, route, path parameters, and key, record the chain in the row, and release a stored credential such as the `uploadToken` only to the chain that stored it. It avoids executing again after a re-mint, and lets another party of the same subject that knows the key and the body read the stored response without its credential.
   - Narrowed by decision 12 on 2026-11-27 (owner's answer to UC-1 of change 0182). Withdrawn: the rotation chain, the `rch` claim, the chain of each source, the IdP chain, the playground chain, the retry cost, and the refusal `principal_chain_required`. Kept: the principal source, now a scope column that identifies no credential; the route, the path parameters, and the effective audience; the scope digest and migration 0208; the replay admission, which decision 18 replaces with the route's authorization checks; the refusals that are not stored; and the clearing of `act` at boundary normalization (AUTHN-I2).
9. **Lenny's client SDK and CLI refresh tokens by self-rotation (IP-8).** The SDKs and the CLI refresh a Lenny-issued token by self-rotation, an exchange of the token for itself with its full `audience` and `scope`, rather than by re-narrowing or minting again, so a retry keeps its rotation chain and its stored result. `docs/client-guide/authentication.md` states the limits: a rotation works only before the token's `exp`; each rotation adds a link to the token's lineage, so a chain ends at `auth.tokenLineageMaxDepth` (TOKX-I26); and a rotation revokes the replaced token, so a token shared between processes cannot be rotated by one of them alone. Step 0178.S5 builds it. Owner's answer to IP-8 in chat, 2026-11-26, option (a), with the words "IP-8: ok". The option not chosen was (b), documenting that clients rotate themselves. Withdrawn by decision 14 on 2026-11-27 (owner's answer to IP-15).
10. **The chain of the service identity and of the development header path (O9).** Defined values: a principal that the service-identity resolver admitted has the chain `svc:` followed by its service-account username (`system:serviceaccount:<namespace>:<name>`), so every token of one account shares a record scope and a retry across the kubelet's token refresh replays; the development header path has the fixed chain `dev`, so every development request of one subject shares a record scope. Owner's answer to item IP-9 of the M3 readiness decision page (open decision O9), 2026-11-27, option (a), with the words "Defined values: `svc:<service-account username>` for the service identity, and the fixed value `dev` for the dev-header path." The options not chosen were (b), both paths refusing a keyed request with `principal_chain_required`, and (c), the service identity taking the value of (a) while the development header path refuses. Withdrawn by decision 15 on 2026-11-27 (owner's answer to IP-16).
11. **The client claim of an identity-provider token's chain (O10).** An operator setting, `auth.oidc.clientIdClaim` (default unset), names the claim of an identity-provider token that carries the client; the gateway reads it ahead of `azp` and `client_id`, and no vendor claim name is built into Lenny. A token that does not carry the named claim falls back to `azp`, then `client_id`, then the sorted `aud`, the order "ahead of" gives. Owner's answer to item IP-10 of the M3 readiness decision page (open decision O10), 2026-11-27, option (a), with the words "An operator setting such as `auth.oidc.clientIdClaim` (default unset) names the claim; the gateway reads it before azp and client_id. No vendor claim name is built in." The option not chosen was (b), keeping the three fallbacks and stating the breadth of the audience fallback. It closes [APICONV-I12](../../issues/closed/APICONV/APICONV-I12-idp-tokens-without-azp-or-client-id-share-a-chain-across-clients.md), which step 0178.S3 builds. Withdrawn by decision 16 on 2026-11-27 (owner's answer to IP-17), which closes APICONV-I12 as obsolete.

13. **Decision 5 and its intent under a scope with no credential identity (O14).** Option (a). The intent of decision 5, that a stored response goes back only to the party that created it, is narrowed to three properties: no stored response carries a credential (change 0182 and IP-8); a replay runs the route's authorization checks (IP-7); and the row records the storing credential's `jti` and client, with the metric `lenny_idempotency_replay_other_credential_total` counting replays by another credential (IP-4). An impersonation bearer is still refused at replay by the creating-caller check. Decision 5's acting-party columns are not rebuilt. Under the scope by subject, a credential of the same subject held by another party that knows the key and the body, such as a second device of the user, a playground bearer, or an `a2a_delegation` token exchanged from the user's token, receives the stored response after the route's authorization checks, which "Limits, and alternatives rejected" states. 18. **Replay admission by the route's authorization checks (O19).** Option (a), IP-7 and IP-8 of `change.md`. The idempotency lookup of every stored REST route, the session-creating routes included, runs inside the request pipeline after `guard`, on the client listener only. A replay runs the permission gate of every route and the ownership check of a session route by construction; the §11.4 gate and the Article 18 restriction on every route; on a session-creating route or tool, the creating-caller check, the §12.8 fence, the §10.6 environment admission, and the §27.5 playground runtime boundary; and on `lenny/delegate_task`, the creating-caller check, the user gate, the ownership check of the parent session, and the §10.6 admission of the target runtime. It does not run the §4.8 policy chain, the §11.1 limits, the delegation policy and budget, or any other check of state, quota, body, or headers. The session core serves the checks through GWSEAM-AdmitRouteReplay and GWSEAM-AdmitToolReplay. `/mcp` stores only the `tools/call` requests of `lenny/create_session`, `lenny/create_and_start_session`, and `lenny/delegate_task`. Step 0178.S4 builds it and closes [APICONV-I13](../../issues/APICONV/APICONV-I13-idempotency-replay-skips-the-pipelines-permission-and-ownership-checks.md) and [APICONV-I14](../../issues/APICONV/APICONV-I14-mcp-read-tool-results-are-stored-and-replayed-without-the-tools-checks.md). It replaces the closed list of decision 8. Owner's answer to IP-19 on the M3 readiness decision page, 2026-11-27, option (a). The options not chosen were (b), keeping the middleware outside the mux and running the pipeline's `guard` from it with the path values set from the route's path parameters, and (c), decision 8's closed list as built, with APICONV-I13 and APICONV-I14 left open.
