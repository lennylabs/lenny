# Decisions: 0178 Idempotency keys scoped by principal

## Open decisions

None.

## Decisions

1. **Idempotency keys are scoped by principal (IK-3).** Idempotency records are scoped by the principal, the tenant together with the user (subject), through a change that edits §11.5 and the store. Owner's answer to IK-3 on the M3 readiness decision page, 2026-11-25, option (a). The option not chosen was (b), keeping the per-tenant scope and documenting it. The question arose from the review of change [0173](../archive/2026-11-25-0173-idempotency-stored-credentials/change.md), whose replay rebuilds an upload token for any caller that holds the key.
2. **User erasure deletes the user's idempotency records (ERASE-I2).** Deleting a user's idempotency records during user erasure, which revision 1 left out of scope, is added to this change as its next step, 0178.S2. Owner's direction in chat, 2026-11-25, with the words "Add the fix for the deletion of idempotency records to the list of next steps." The deletion finds the records by the subject that step 0178.S1 adds.
3. **The scope of a bearer bound to a session (O1).** When the principal carries a `SessionID`, as a session-capability bearer and the bearer of a delegated child do, the record scope includes it, so the scope is the tenant, the subject, and the session for such a bearer, and the tenant and the subject otherwise. The session id is an extra column of the primary key of `idempotency_keys`, `session_id`, which holds the empty string for an ordinary user token, because a primary-key column cannot be NULL. The option not chosen was (b), keeping the scope at the tenant and the subject for every principal, as decision 1 states it. Owner's answer to IP-1 (open decision O1) on the M3 readiness decision page, 2026-11-26, option (a).
4. **Coverage waiver for 0178.S1 and 0178.S2.** The floor of 80% is waived for `pkg/idempotency/idempotencymw/pgstore/pgstore.go` (57 changed lines), which only the component tests reach, and for the test fixture `tests/testinfra/ownerprincipal/ownerprincipal.go` (16 lines), which the contract and security tests use. The measured coverage was 62.6% (129 of 206 lines) on gate run `e916a235`, and 97% (129 of 133 lines) without the two files. Issue GATE-I14 records that the coverage check measures the unit tier alone. Owner's answer to IP-W in chat, 2026-11-26, option (a), with the words "IP-W: waive".
5. **The acting party in the scope (O2).** A bearer whose `act` claim names another party files its idempotency records apart from the user's: the scope gains the actor, `act.sub` and `act.session_id`, each empty when absent, as a further primary-key column of `idempotency_keys`, resolved from `authmw.Principal.Act`. An ordinary user's scope keeps empty actor values. Step 0178.S3 builds it, and whether the platform-admin impersonation bearer and the playground session-capability bearer carry an `act` claim, or need their own marker to be scoped apart, is settled while building that step against the tree. Owner's answer to IP-2 in chat, 2026-11-26, option (a) on the M3 readiness decision page (option (b) above), with the words "IP-2: (a)". The options not chosen were keeping the scope and stating the limit in §11.5, and filing an impersonation bearer under its own marker and a playground bearer under its origin.
6. **Change 0138 R5 keeps the bound session and the acting party (O3).** R5 of [0138](../0138-rest-only-client-api/change.md) and its step 0138.S1 keep the bound session (decision 3) and the acting party (decision 5) in the scope of an idempotency key, beside the dimensions R5 adds. Owner's answer to IP-3 in chat, 2026-11-26, option (a), with the words "IP-3: (a) + the acting party from IP-2, right?". The option not chosen was (b), letting 0138 replace the session dimension with its own.
