### 0144.S1 One route
- status: Planned

Everything above: the removal of the replay route; the admission and row-build extraction with the gates on the derive's final pool; the transcript sender and delivery state on the REST send path and the `from=client` read; `origin` and `cwd` on the stored plan, the schema, the proto, and the mapping; the session row fields; "every bind stores a plan" on the create, create-and-start, and delegation paths; the resume of a derived session before its own checkpoint; the plan check function; the storage reservation and the upload copy; and `content_stop_epochs`, `planOriginRuntimes`, and the content stop check. Whether the step splits into smaller steps is open decision 11 in `decisions.md`.

Parts of the step wait on other steps, which `depends-on` marks with `(part)`:

- The `from=client` read and the transcript writes of "Reading client messages" land with or after 0135.S1 of [`0135-message-delivery`](../0135-message-delivery/steps.md#steps), which adds the `session_messages` sender and delivery-state columns with their migration under MD-3 **Records** and replaces the executor's `Send` with `Deliver` (consistency conflict client-api-sessions-C10).
- The copy of the pending client tool calls on a `from: latest` derive, the `422 CLIENT_TOOLS_UNSUPPORTED` check of T11, and `toolResults` on the `from=client` read land with or after 0122.S1 of [`0122-client-tools`](../0122-client-tools/steps.md#steps), which adds the pending set and the results (consistency conflict client-api-sessions-C7). This step's rewrite of the derive handler carries them.
- The withdrawal of SD-P4, under which a derive meets no supervisor check, lands with or after 0147.S3 of [`0147-tenant-derived-runtimes`](../0147-tenant-derived-runtimes/steps.md#steps), which runs setup commands at the slot UID through the slot's supervisor, and after 0119.S1 of [`0119-agent-runtimes-only`](../0119-agent-runtimes-only/steps.md#steps) and 0118.S1 of [`0118-agent-pod-layout`](../0118-agent-pod-layout/steps.md#steps), which remove the `type: mcp` runtime and the embedded deployment model whose pods run no supervisor. Until then a file the source's agent kept could reach an operator's setup command at the adapter's UID with the pod's service account token, as F-7.5.17 records.

The rest is conditional, and each design adds its rule to the checks when it lands, before or after this step. A plan with no clone depends on no other step. A plan with a clone needs the [egress design](../0127-egress-and-git/change.md)'s pod-side clone, as any `gitClone` does. The restore through the slot's broker, `restore-complete`, and `materialize` arrive with 0153.S6 of [`0153-workspace-materialization`](../0153-workspace-materialization/steps.md#steps), which depends on this step and owns the tests of the helper, broker, and slot-UID parts. The `registered` check of DR-4 at the post-chain point arrives with 0147.S4, which lands it with or after proposal AR-D (0119.S4) of [`0119-agent-runtimes-only`](../0119-agent-runtimes-only/steps.md#steps). The placement checks arrive with [`0136-placement`](../0136-placement/steps.md#steps). "Warm stores on the target", `coveredStores`, and `withheldStores` arrive with [DR-11](../0147-tenant-derived-runtimes/change.md#warm-stores). SD-1 also extracts the plan check function that [DR-9](../0147-tenant-derived-runtimes/change.md#dr-9-default-git-clones) (0147.S7) calls. The `PreRoute` payload with `kind: derive` comes from the first proposal of [`0141-runtime-routing`](../0141-runtime-routing/steps.md#steps): if SD-1 lands first, that proposal adds `kind: derive` to the derive path, and otherwise SD-1 adopts the payload it landed. Revision selection arrives with [`0140-runtime-revisions`](../0140-runtime-revisions/steps.md#steps), the account link check with [UC-2](../0151-user-credential-delivery/change.md#flow), the refusal of `deferConnectors` with proposal CR-A of [`0124-connector-removal`](../0124-connector-removal/steps.md#steps), and the pack reference and `checkpointing_disabled` with [IC-5](../0131-incremental-checkpoint/change.md#ic-5-restore-derive-download-and-seal) and [IC-8](../0131-incremental-checkpoint/change.md#ic-8-checkpoint-policy-interval-and-change-tracker).

"Stops": this step adds the `content_stop_epochs` table, `planOriginRuntimes` and its copy, and the content stop check. The counter increments and the composition append land with the steps that add the mechanisms they ride on, as "Stops" in `change.md` states: the grant counter and the append with 0147.S4 (DR-4), the retirement the check reads with 0147.S10 (DR-6), and the security counter with whichever of 0147.S11 (DR-7) and 0142.S2 (SO-2) lands second.

0119.S2 also removes the derive's execution-mode equality check, `requireReplayRuntimeCompatible`, `INCOMPATIBLE_RUNTIME`, and the test `tests/contract/rest_sessions/replay_incompatible_runtime_contract_test.go`, and this step removes the replay route that holds part of that code. Whichever of the two steps lands second skips the removal the first made. 0119.S2 closes the `executionMode` half of F-15.1.76, and this step closes its `runtimeRef` half.

Under decision 8 of [0117](../0117-gateway-package-layout/decisions.md), this step names the gateway seam operations whose mechanism it changes. The transcript writes of "Reading client messages" sit behind `GWSEAM-DeliverHeld` and behind the `Deliver` path with which 0135.S1 replaces `GWSEAM-SendMessages`. The writes change no operation's method, parameters, returns, or summary, so the step edits `spec/interfaces/gateway-seams.md` and `pkg/gateway/seam` only if the build finds that one of these must change, and the step's digest records the addition of 0144 to decision 8's list of affected changes, as the amendment of 2026-11-19 states.
