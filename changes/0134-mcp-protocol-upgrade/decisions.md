# Decisions: 0134 MCP protocol upgrade

## Open decisions

O8 to O11 are Lenny's. Where this file cites "O8 of `0138-rest-only-client-api`", it names that change's decision, which is unrelated to O8 below.

- **O8. The status of 0134.S1 and 0134.S2 in Lenny's records.** Both steps are `Planned`, carry the lanes `[specification, code, documentation]`, and appear on the board, for work that the banner assigns to the MCP adapter project. Their content, the dual-era gateway `/mcp`, the reply consumer for `elicitation/create`, and the removal of the WebSocket transport, is code that 0138.S6 removes, and the change cannot reach `Implemented` while they are `Planned`. Options: (a) mark both `Cancelled`, record the reason "moves to `lenny-adapter-mcp` under O8 of `0138-rest-only-client-api`" in `review-history.md`, and keep their text; (b) move them to a subfolder of this change, which the owner's words in O8 of 0138 also permit; (c) leave them `Planned`. Recommendation: (a). Cancelling a step deletes no text, so the owner's "Don't delete anything" holds, and the board treats a `Cancelled` step as done. It is the same question as O21 of `0120-anthropic-messages-api`. Blocks: 0134.S1, 0134.S2, the board, and the status of 0134. Other changes: 0138 and 0120. (raised by the M3 readiness review, 2026-11-19; sources: 0134-F2, client-api-sessions-C13)
- **O9. The handshake-era session, the `Origin` rule, and event subscription of the management server.** "MP-7" took these from "MP-1", which moves to the MCP adapter project and holds two session mechanisms: revision 6's Redis record with the gateway Helm value of D5, which is not bound to a subject, and the signed token of O5 (a) of `0138-rest-only-client-api`. `pkg/ops/mcp/server.go` has no session handling and no `Origin` check today. "MP-7" replaces `notifications/subscribe` with `subscriptions/listen` on the stateless era alone, which leaves a `2025-11-25` client with no subscription, and it names neither the notification that carries the events nor the scope and tenant filter of §25.5. Options for the session: (a) a signed, self-contained `Mcp-Session-Id` that holds the negotiated version, the client's capabilities, a digest of the bearer's subject, and an expiry, as O5 (a) of 0138 chose for the adapter, with no Redis record and no new setting; (b) a Redis record with a `lenny-ops` setting for its idle expiry, bound to the bearer's subject; (c) the stateless era alone on the management server, with no handshake-era session. For the rest: a request whose `Origin` the server does not accept receives HTTP 403, and event subscription works on both eras, with the notification method named and the §25.5 scope and tenant filter applied to each caller. Recommendation: (a), with the `Origin` refusal and subscription on both eras. Blocks: 0134.S4. (raised by the M3 readiness review, 2026-11-19; sources: 0134-F4)
- **O10. The version rule and the adoption window for Lenny's surfaces.** MPR-4, which states the rule that names each version with its URL and check date, and the window that O6 settled move to the MCP adapter project with the gateway's `/mcp`. "Specification changes" placed both in §15.2 and §15.5, which leave Lenny's scope under 0138. MPR-8 keeps the naming rule for the reference bridge. Open: whether the naming rule also covers the management server, whether O6's window applies to the bridge and the management server when MCP publishes a new version, and which page states them. Options: (a) both apply to the bridge and the management server, stated once on `COMPAT` (`spec/cross-cutting/upgrade-and-compatibility.md`) and cited from `RTPSDK` and `OPSMCP`; (b) both apply, stated on `RTPSDK` for the bridge and on `OPSMCP` for the management server; (c) the naming rule applies with no window, so the release that adopts a new MCP version drops the oldest on Lenny's surfaces. Recommendation: (a), with the two-version rule of D1 and O6's window counted in releases. "RP-7" of `0139-runtime-protocol` cites the gateway's two-version rule and needs the answer. Blocks: 0134.S5, 0134.S3, and 0134.S4. Other change: 0139. (raised by the M3 readiness review, 2026-11-19; sources: 0134-F5)
- **O11. The SDK spike of O2 after the split.** O2 has the spike test the gateway's auth middleware, Lenny's error envelope, the attach stream, and per-tenant routing, all of which move to the MCP adapter project, and says that "its result decides one approach for every surface". The Go SDK cannot serve the Python and TypeScript bridges, and the spike's criteria for the bridge and for `lenny-ops` are unstated. Options: (a) keep the spike, re-scoped for Lenny: the Go SDK hosting `lenny-mcp-bridge` over stdio and in process at both eras, and `pkg/ops/mcp` behind the `lenny-ops` auth middleware, with the Python and TypeScript bridges hand-written or built on the official MCP SDKs of those languages, and "one approach" read per language; (b) no spike for Lenny's surfaces, which stay hand-written; (c) adopt the Go SDK for the Go surfaces without a spike. Recommendation: (a). Blocks: 0134.S3 and 0134.S4. (raised by the M3 readiness review, 2026-11-19; sources: 0134-F6)

## Decisions

> **Revision 9.** [`0124-connector-removal`](../0124-connector-removal/change.md) removes connectors under the owner's decision of 2026-11-15 ("Yes, let's drop connectors."). A statement in this section about connectors, `connector:<id>`, the connector client, connector links, `deferredToolDiscovery`, `tool_progress`, or `toolProgress` that the edits of revision 9 did not rewrite is withdrawn and kept as the record. The platform statements stand.

Under O8 of [`0138-rest-only-client-api`](../0138-rest-only-client-api/change.md), D4, D5, and O4 to O7 move to the MCP adapter project, and D3, D6, and O1 are Lenny's. D1, D2, and O2 apply to both: the adapter takes the half that governs its client-facing endpoint, and Lenny keeps the half that governs the reference bridge and the management server. O3 governed the connector client and is moot since revision 9.
