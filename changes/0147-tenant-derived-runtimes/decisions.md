# Decisions: 0147 Tenant-derived runtimes

## Owner's choice

On 2026-11-04 the owner chose this design over a tenant-owned pool on an unchanged base, in these words: "I want derived runtimes for the two reasons you state. I want tenants to define their own default files and git clones." The two reasons are that transparent clients of a shared runtime keep working when an environment adds a deny, and that a tenant admin creates runtimes. The alternative stays in git history as `designs/tenant-owned-pools-design.md`, revision 5, at commit `7a72e0b65`.

The owner then answered "Default clones: yes" and "Default setup commands: yes", and added: "I think we need to add settings to control the min number of pods per tenant to reserve capacity. This should only be allowed if the platform admin/operator allow it". DR-9 and DR-10 state the clones and the reservation, and "Tenant defaults" states the commands.

On warming, the owner asked for "warming up the clone ahead of time", and then confirmed that draft proposal 0111 is what that meant: "This design should build on top of 0111, or replace 0111 where it makes sense. Add a note to the design doc for the record". "Warm stores" states the design and "Relationship to draft proposal 0111" holds that note. On the write scope of a default clone, the owner observed that "the client may not know what the repo is called, since it's not declared in the session but the pool", and then accepted the recommended `write` field. On derive, the owner wrote: "ideally we pick up the exact state in which the source session was", and "Whether the repo is read-only or write-only should be irrelevant. I don't think we should provide `defaultCloneCommits` as an option in the derive or replay APIs." The owner then merged derive and replay into one route with two modes, which [`0144-session-derive`](../0144-session-derive/change.md) states.

## Decisions

The owner has settled every decision below, and the design above states each one. The owner settled decision 4: tenants can define default files, setup commands, and git clones, so its alternative of no tenant defaults is gone, and the owner chose the grant flags in the words "separate flags, off by default". The owner settled decision 17 and decision 20 as recommended, the latter in the words "go with dedicated pools". The owner settled decision 16 as recommended, in the words "go with your recommendation", and settled decisions 22 and 27 on 2026-11-04. The owner settled decisions 1, 3, 6, 7, and 8 as recommended on 2026-11-04, each in the word "ok". The owner settled decisions 9 to 12 as recommended the same day, in the words "go with your recommendations", and decisions 5 and 28, in the words "yes" and "makes sense, yes". The owner settled decision 2 as recommended the same day, after considering an additional grant covering all tenants, in the words "no all-tenant grant. Since grants can be configured via API, it's not a big deal to have grants that are specific to tenants". The owner settled decisions 29 and 30 as recommended on 2026-11-08, in the words "go with your recommendations". The owner settled decisions 31 to 37 on 2026-11-08, after an assessment of the operator's `gitRepository` entry against the warm clone and a check of its open points, and settled decision 38 as recommended and decision 39 later that day. The owner settled decision 40 on 2026-11-09, during the review that compared every design with the others, and decisions 42 and 43 on 2026-11-14.
