- Revision 2, dated 2026-11-15, applies the adversarial security review of revision 1, kept as [`evidence/service-account-credentials-design/security-review.md`](evidence/security-review.md), and the owner's answer of that day to its summary, "O1-O7: go with your recommendations". It changes these rules.
  - **Finding 1, the exchange JWT sent anywhere.** The vendor profiles post only to fixed endpoints with fixed audiences built from validated identifiers, the generic profiles take `issuer` alone and bind the audience to the issuer whose discovered token endpoint they call, reserved vendor audiences and hosts are refused on the generic profiles, `verify` and `retry` post only to the recorded endpoint, and an `issuerExchange` type needs its record on `tokenTrust.allowedRecords` (SAR-10, SAR-13, SAP-3, and SAP-12).
  - **Finding 2, subject collision.** SA-6 places service and `agent` callers' Lenny subjects under `~svc~` and `~agt~`, enforced at the identity boundary, at the writes that name a subject, and at token issuance (SAR-11 and SAP-10).
  - **Finding 3, the shared federation issuer (O1).** Exchange JWTs move to the exchange issuer `https://<lenny>/exchange/<tenant-id>` with its own RS256 key set, which reverses revision 1's recommendation. The federation issuer signs federation tokens alone again in the egress and connector removal designs, and keeps RS256 because federation serves the Azure SDKs at Entra (SAP-4).
  - **Finding 4, persons under `issuerExchange` (O3).** `principalTypes` is fixed at `[service]` on that source.
  - **Finding 5, AWS through `metadata` (O6).** Its own grant, `userCredentialMetadataDelivery`, a fixed `DurationSeconds` of 900, an optional session policy, exposure records, `lockEgressToHosts`, `leakScan`, per-slot attribution, and the blast radius stated (SAP-13).
  - **Finding 6, self-writes.** Every changing route refuses a caller writing its own row with `403 USER_CREDENTIAL_SELF_WRITE` (SAR-12 and SAP-11).
  - **Findings 7 to 10.** The client assertion's audience names the server called, and one key serves one row; CR-2 gives `agent` callers `principal_type: agent`; the exchange key is published one day before use, an emergency rotation's refusals stay `unavailable`, and `ExpiredToken` maps to `unavailable`; the provider's error text is bounded, redacted, and escaped; and audit events name key generation, activation, and discard, `retry`, verification, self-write refusals, and exchange key rotation, with `sessionOrigin` on `credential.mint`.
  - **External claims.** AWS's `InvalidIdentityToken` is cited to the IAM page, which states the key retrieval case, beside the STS page's wording, and Google's 127-character `google.subject` limit was confirmed on the overview page.
  - **O7.** `registered` types default to `[human]`, which reverses revision 1's `[human, service]`.
  - It applies its changes to [`0151-user-credential-delivery`](../0151-user-credential-delivery/change.md) revision 40, [`0127-egress-and-git`](../0127-egress-and-git/change.md) revision 92, [`0124-connector-removal`](../0124-connector-removal/change.md) revision 7, [`0152-user-git-identity`](../0152-user-git-identity/change.md) revision 45, [`0149-tenant-scoping-identity`](../0149-tenant-scoping-identity/change.md) revision 42, and [`0142-security-oversight`](../0142-security-oversight/change.md) revision 58. No decision is open.
