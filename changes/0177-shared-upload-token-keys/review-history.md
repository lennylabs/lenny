The owner directed that the dead-letter redaction receipt signer be fixed in this change and reuse its mechanism where possible (Owner's direction in chat, 2026-11-25), recorded as decision 2. Issues [UPLOAD-I1](../../issues/UPLOAD/UPLOAD-I1-upload-token-keys-and-consumed-state-per-replica.md) and [AUDIT-I2](../../issues/AUDIT/AUDIT-I2-redaction-receipt-signing-key-per-process.md) were filed, and step 0177.S1 closes both. "Problem" and "Scope" gain the receipt signer. The signing-key store of SK-2 is keyed by purpose, with a policy per purpose for the algorithm, the KEK alias, the rotation interval, and how long a retired key keeps verifying, so it serves upload tokens and, when O3 chooses the store, redaction receipts. SK-1 gains a row for the receipt keys, which have to verify for `audit.gdprRetentionDays` and so cannot live in Redis alone; O1 notes that this strengthens Postgres only under O3 (b) or (c). SK-4 states the receipt purpose's failure behavior, and SK-6 describes the receipt signer and the chain verifier's signature check. Reading §12.8 step 14 showed that it asks for a KMS-held key with a public key for external verifiers, which an HMAC key from the shared store does not give, and that its reference to §11.7 genesis-nonce signing names a mechanism §11.7 does not define; this raised open decision O3, with KMS signing recommended. The title changed to name both signers. O1 and O2 stay open, and the status stays `Draft`.
