### 0177.S2 The receipt key set, the verification time of replaced keys, and the check against new_hash
- status: Building
- lanes: [specification, code, documentation]
- depends-on: [0177.S1]
- closes: [UPLOAD-I2, AUDIT-I5]

Decisions 7, 8, and 9. First the specification: §7.1 **`uploadToken` format and security properties** states that a replaced key verifies for the stale bound plus the longer of the overlap window and the token lifetime (decision 7), and §12.8 step 14 states the check of a redacted row against the receipt's `new_hash` (decision 8) and the set of receipt keys a verifier trusts, with the setting of retired receipt keys (decision 9). Then `signingkeys.UploadTokenPolicy` and its wiring in `cmd/lenny-gateway/token_signing.go` derive the verification time from the stale bound, the overlap window, and `maxCreatedStateTimeoutSeconds`; the chain verifier and the periodic receipt scan in `pkg/audit/integrity` compare a redacted row's content hash with `new_hash`; the erasure path writes a chained receipt for a second redaction of a row if a row can be redacted twice; and the chain verifier and the periodic receipt scan trust the retired receipt keys the new setting lists, read through the `kms.Provider` lookup by key identifier (decision 9). The tests: a token minted by a replica at its stale bound verifies on a peer until it expires; a token lifetime above the overlap window verifies across a rotation; a redacted row rewritten again reads `broken`; a legitimate second redaction verifies; a receipt signed by a retired key on the list verifies after two rotations; and a receipt signed by a key outside the trusted set reads `broken`. Then `docs/operator-guide/security.md`, `docs/operator-guide/configuration.md`, and `docs/operator-guide/audit-ocsf.md`. The step closes [UPLOAD-I2](../../issues/UPLOAD/UPLOAD-I2-stale-replica-mints-tokens-peers-refuse-before-expiry.md) and [AUDIT-I5](../../issues/AUDIT/AUDIT-I5-receipt-check-ignores-the-signed-new-hash.md).
