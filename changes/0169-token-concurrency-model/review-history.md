"Model results" records the results and the counterexamples R1 to R6 and the gaps G1 to G3. Open decisions 10 to 14 are raised: the undetected wait of 0168's impersonation step, TOKX-I13 under 0168's design, a bearer row without a start record, the rule 7 window and the unlocked caller check, and the validation fallback read.
