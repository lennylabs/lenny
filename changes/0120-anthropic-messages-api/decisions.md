# Decisions: 0120 Anthropic Messages API compatibility

## Open decisions

- **O14. The in-tree OpenAI translators until 0138.S6, and what 0120.S1 closes.** 0120.S1 changes the executor to one envelope per delivery call ("AM-4", "Executor"). The OpenAI translators stay in the tree until 0138.S6 removes them, and they call `Send` with several messages (`pkg/gateway/environment/translator/openai_chat.go` and `open_responses.go`). The program has to say whether those surfaces keep working in the window, and 0120.S1 and 0138.S6 both listed F-15.1.80 to F-15.1.83 in full. Options: (a) the surfaces that leave may break from the first step that changes what they call; 0120.S1 edits no translator handler, closes F-15.1.80 (part, the executor half), and 0138.S6 closes the translator half of F-15.1.80 and F-15.1.81 to F-15.1.83 by removal; (b) every earlier step keeps them working, so 0120.S1 also passes the two handlers' history as `conversation.entries` and closes F-15.1.80 and F-15.1.81, and 0138.S6 closes F-15.1.82 and F-15.1.83; (c) a new early step of 0138 removes them ahead of 0135.S1. Recommendation: (a), which the banner and O8 of 0138 already state for this change, and which the steps file follows until the owner answers. It is option A of the program-wide question that `0138-rest-only-client-api` also records, and O17 of [`0135-message-delivery`](../0135-message-delivery/decisions.md) records the same question for 0135.S1 and 0135.S2 with the same recommendation. Blocks: 0120.S1, 0122.S1, and 0138.S6. Other changes: 0135 (O17) and 0138. (raised by the M3 readiness review, 2026-11-19; sources: 0120-F1, client-api-sessions-C12)
- **O15. The order of 0120.S1 and 0135.S1 on the executor.** 0135.S1 replaces `Send` with `Deliver(ctx, sessionID, []Envelope)` and removes `GWSEAM-SendMessages` (decision 92 of 0117), and 0120.S1 changes what `Send` writes. Neither step orders them, so the one that lands second rewrites the call the other changed. Options: (a) 0120.S1 depends on 0135.S1 and adds the `conversation` member to the envelope that `Deliver` writes; (b) 0120.S1 lands first on today's `Send` and reshapes `GWSEAM-SendMessages`, and 0135.S1 carries the member through `Deliver`. Recommendation: (a). It writes the executor change once, and it delays no step that does not already wait on 0135.S1: 0135.S2 depends on 0135.S1, 0138.S1 depends on 0135.S5, and the consistency review recommends adding 0135.S1 to 0122.S1. Blocks: 0120.S1. (raised by the M3 readiness review, 2026-11-19; sources: 0120-F11)
- **O16. The validation rules for a client-written `conversation` that "AM-4", "Validation" leaves open.** Under R15 of 0138 a REST client writes the member. "Validation" states the refusal of a member with no marked `user` entry and of an `input` that differs from it, and accepts `instructions` as written. Still open: an entry whose `role` is outside `user`, `assistant`, and `system`; unmarked entries on a send to a session that already ran turns; and unknown keys in `parameters`. Options: (a) refuse an unknown role and an unknown `parameters` key with `400 VALIDATION_ERROR` naming the member, and deliver unmarked entries as sent, because they are the client's assertion; (b) refuse an unknown role, pass unknown `parameters` keys as hints, and refuse unmarked entries on a session that already ran turns. Recommendation: (a). Blocks: 0120.S1 and 0138.S1. (raised by the M3 readiness review, 2026-11-19; sources: 0120-F3, client-api-sessions-C3)
- **O17. Enforced parameters on REST: the refusal, and the path back from the runtime.** R15 of 0138 applies AM-3's enforced-parameter rules, AM-3 moves to the adapter, and RP-2 of `0139-runtime-protocol` says the parameters are not enforced for a runtime without `conversationParameters`, while AM-3 refuses `stop_sequences` and `thinking.display` for such a runtime. No change defines how a runtime reports that `maxTokens` or a stop sequence ended its output, and no REST field reports that the gateway rendered a transcript and dropped the hints. `parameters.outputSchema` has no Lenny refusal once a REST send returns before the turn ends. Options: (a) keep enforced parameters: a REST send with a non-default enforced parameter that the chosen revision does not declare is refused whole with `400 VALIDATION_ERROR`, the end-of-turn `message` gains a stop member such as `stop: {reason, sequence}` exposed on the turn resource of 0138 (R3), the send receipt or the turn resource reports transcript rendering and the dropped keys, and output schema validation stays with the adapter; (b) remove `conversationParameters` from v1 and pass every parameter as a hint, retiring AMR-16. Recommendation: (a), because RP-2 of 0139 and R15 of 0138 already carry the capability. Blocks: 0120.S1, 0138.S1, and the RP-2 row of 0139.S1. Other changes: 0138 and 0139. (raised by the M3 readiness review, 2026-11-19; sources: 0120-F3, 0120-F4)
- **O18. The size bound for a send that carries `conversation`.** The tree caps a REST body at 1 MiB (`MaxJSONBodyBytes` in `pkg/gateway/core/session/sessionserver/json_body.go`, applied by the message send and create-and-start), AM-1's 32 MiB cap moves to the adapter, R15 states none, and the Go runtime SDK caps one frame at 50 MiB. An adapter that forwards a history above 1 MiB is refused on every cold turn, and a raised cap with no bound lets a client send a history the runtime cannot read. Options: (a) an operator setting for the body cap of a send that carries `conversation`, default 32 MiB, a count limit on `entries`, each part following the §28.5.3 inline and staging thresholds, and `413` above the cap; (b) keep 1 MiB and require the client to stage large parts as blobs before the send. Recommendation: (a), with the default and the count limit set by the owner; 32 MiB stays below the SDK frame cap. Blocks: 0120.S1 and 0138.S1. (raised by the M3 readiness review, 2026-11-19; sources: 0120-F5)
- **O19. Whether the session transcript records the unmarked entries and `instructions`.** "AM-4", "Custody and redelivery" keeps the whole member with the message under custody. The transcript decides what erasure, retention, derive, and a `conversation_only` resume include. Options: (a) the transcript records the marked entries only, and the stored message keeps the rest and is erased with it; (b) the transcript records every entry and `instructions`, so the history the client asserted is part of the session's record. Recommendation: (a). Blocks: 0120.S1. (raised by the M3 readiness review, 2026-11-19; sources: 0120-F6)
- **O20. Whether `PreRoute` interceptors see `conversation`.** The `PreRoute` payload of RT-2 in `0141-runtime-routing` carries `input` and no `conversation`, and the transcript rendering happens after revision selection, so content a client places in `conversation.entries` or `conversation.instructions` of a create-and-start request bypasses an interceptor that checks the creation input. For a `one_shot` runtime the creation input is the only client input. Options: (a) the payload carries `conversation` as a protected member that an interceptor reads and may answer with `REJECT`, and rendering happens after the chain on the payload the chain returned; (b) the payload carries it and `MODIFY` may change it; (c) interceptors do not see it, stated as a limit. Recommendation: (a), as this change's instruction to 0141 asked. Blocks: 0120.S1 or 0138.S1, and the step of 0141 that builds RT-2. Other change: 0141. (raised by the M3 readiness review, 2026-11-19; sources: 0120-F9)
- **O21. The status of 0120.S2 and 0120.S3 in Lenny's records.** Both steps are `Planned` for work the banner assigns to the Anthropic adapter project, 0122.S2 depends on both, and the change cannot reach `Implemented` while they are `Planned`. Options: (a) mark both `Cancelled` with a note that the adapter repository builds them under 0138.S5, keep their text, and remove them from 0122.S2's `depends-on`; (b) keep them `Planned` and build the adapter in the gateway before 0138.S6 removes it; (c) add a "moved" marker to the step format. Recommendation: (a). Cancelling a step deletes no text, so the owner's "Don't delete anything" holds. Blocks: 0120.S2, 0120.S3, 0122.S2, and the status of 0120. Other changes: 0122, and 0134, whose 0134.S1 and 0134.S2 carry the same pattern. (raised by the M3 readiness review, 2026-11-19; sources: 0120-F2)
- **O22. Cold continuation of a client tool round trip over REST.** 0138 makes every provider adapter a REST client that continues cold by creating a session with the history in `conversation.entries`, while 0122 states that REST has no cold path with history, refuses `toolResults` on a session with no pending calls, and under its O4 refuses client tools on `one_shot` runtimes. Options: (a) add the REST cold path: a new session's first message may carry `toolResults` that answer the calls of its last `assistant` entry, and 0122 withdraws its O4; (b) add the cold path and keep 0122's O4 for `one_shot` runtimes; (c) no REST cold path, and 0138's RO-10 states that adapters cannot continue a tool round trip cold. Recommendation: (a). Blocks: 0120.S1, 0122.S1, and 0138.S1. Other changes: 0122 and 0138. (raised by the M3 readiness review, 2026-11-19; sources: client-api-sessions-C1)

## Decisions

Under O8 of [`0138-rest-only-client-api`](../0138-rest-only-client-api/change.md), the decisions that govern "AM-1" to "AM-3" and "AM-5" to "AM-10" move to the Anthropic adapter project with those sections. D2 and O3 still govern Lenny as they apply to "AM-4", and O9 governs Lenny's aliases through RT-4, as that design's inventory lists it. The second sentence of D2, and the clause of O3 that moves the OpenAI translators onto the same input, govern the translators and move with them to the OpenAI adapter projects; in Lenny, conversation input replaces one turn per message in the executor ("AM-4", "Executor").

Settled by project rules:

| ID | Decision | Basis |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| D1 | The adapter is a built-in adapter in the `ExternalAdapterRegistry`, beside MCP and the OpenAI translators, and creates sessions through the shared create-and-start service. | §15 "Three tiers of pluggability" and the single-shot compute model. One creation path keeps admission and the post-chain point uniform. |
| D2 | The design adds no compatibility mode, legacy flag, or migration path for Lenny's own surfaces. The OpenAI translators change their history delivery in place (O3). | The project rule against compatibility shims before a first deployment (`feedback_no_backward_compat`). |
| D3 | A request field or content block that Lenny cannot honor is refused with `400 invalid_request_error` naming it, unless it is a hint with no effect on output semantics, such as `cache_control`. The existing translators' practice of decoding a request and dropping unknown fields is not copied. | Fail closed on behaviour a client relies on. A silently ignored `tools` array would return prose to a client that expects `tool_use`. |
| D4 | Every claim about the Messages API in this design comes from the pages under "Sources", read on 2026-11-09. | `feedback_verify_external_standard_versions`. |

Settled by the owner on 2026-11-09. The M labels are the adversarial review's findings that the owner answered together with an open decision:

| ID | Decision |
| :-- | :-- |
| O1 | The client presents a Lenny bearer in `Authorization: Bearer` or in `x-api-key`. The auth middleware normalizes `x-api-key` into the request's credential and strips the header. A value that starts with `sk-ant-` receives a specific `401 authentication_error` that never echoes the value. Conflicting values in the two headers receive `401 authentication_error` ("AM-2"). |
| O2 and M1 | Conversation state is kept by warm continuation on a keyed digest, with a cold fallback. The digest is HMAC-SHA256 and includes `metadata.user_id`. The canonical form follows RFC 8785. Consecutive messages of one role, and adjacent text blocks, are merged before hashing. `lenny.labels` is excluded from the digest. A retry reattaches to its running turn through an in-flight join. The beta `thread` parameter stays refused, and once it is stable a thread identifier maps to a session as `previous_response_id` does ("AM-5"). |
| O3 | Cold turns deliver the conversation as structured input to runtimes that declare it, and as a transcript to others, and the OpenAI translators move onto the same input. The `conversation` member carries ordered entries with the new input marked, so a trailing system message has a place. The beta per-message fields are refused ("AM-3" and "AM-4"). |
| O4, M3, and M6 | No open turns: revision 2's AM-D is removed, and a turn ends inside its request. A long turn uses streaming. Client tools come from [`0122-client-tools`](../0122-client-tools/change.md), which replaces revision 2's AM-7 bridge. `lenny/request_input` and elicitation toward the client are refused at once on this surface, and the adapter declares `SupportsElicitation: false` ("AM-6" and "AM-7"). |
| O5 and M4 | The runtime declares which parameters it honours. A non-default `stop_sequences` or `thinking.display` for a runtime that does not declare it is refused. `effort` and `thinking` pass as hints. `max_tokens` is per message: it caps the turn's visible output, only for a runtime that declares it, and never maps to the session's budget ("AM-3"). |
| O6 | `GET /v1/models` chooses its format by `anthropic-version` or by `?adapter=anthropic-messages`, sends `Vary: anthropic-version`, dispatches `GET /v1/models/{id}` the same way, and reports a capability it cannot vouch for as false or null ("AM-9"). |
| O7 and M5 | The adapter accepts `2026-10-21` and `2023-06-01`, and refuses an unknown newer version with an error that lists the supported ones ("AM-2"). |
| O8 | Removed with open turns. Revision 2's `pause_turn` wait limit and `anthropicMessages.maxResponseWaitSeconds` go. |
| O10 | The `lenny` object is create-only. Its `labels` are excluded from the digest, and a warm turn ignores the object ("AM-3"). |
| M2 | A held session follows the Open Responses hold rule of §15 "Open Responses conversation sessions", and the adapter adds no timer of its own ("AM-5"). Superseded on 2026-11-14 by the simplified O3 of [`0138-rest-only-client-api`](../0138-rest-only-client-api/change.md), which the owner answered "yes": no session is held, and "Session lifetime" states the rule. |
| M7 | `anthropic-beta` is accepted and ignored. Field-level refusals enforce behaviour ("AM-2"). |
| MD O3 | Decided on [`0135-message-delivery`](../0135-message-delivery/change.md), and binding here. A message from another session to a held session is delivered on arrival. Client tools are offered only in turns a client request caused, and a turn that no client request caused never appears in a Messages response, whose content is the output of the client's own turn. The conversation digest is unaffected ("AM-5", "Messages from other sessions"). |
| M8 | This design asks [`0149-tenant-scoping-identity`](../0149-tenant-scoping-identity/change.md) for a `jwt-bearer` grant on `/v1/oauth/token`, so the SDKs' workload identity federation can obtain a Lenny bearer ("Instructions for reviewers of other designs"). |

The options and reasoning of O11 and O12, kept as a record:

| ID | Question | Options | Recommendation |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| O11 | How the adapter answers a turn that ends with `BUDGET_EXHAUSTED` under [`0133-llm-upstreams-and-cost-budgets`](../0133-llm-upstreams-and-cost-budgets/change.md) ("AM-8"). | (a) `429 rate_limit_error`, with `details.limitType` naming the budget's scope and `x-should-retry: false`.
