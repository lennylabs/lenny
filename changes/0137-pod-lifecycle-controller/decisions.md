## Open decisions

D6 settled O3, and D7 settled O4. "PLC-2" **Names.** holds the names table that O3 presented. The questions below are open:

- **O5. Which controller creates and deletes `PodSet` objects.** This design gives every `PodSet` spec write to the PoolScalingController ("PLC-2" **The merge.**, PLCR-4, PLCR-17), and so do §4.6.3, the chart (`charts/lenny/templates/controller-rbac.yaml` grants the WarmPoolController only `get`, `list`, and `watch` on `podsets`, and `pool-scaling-controller-rbac.yaml` grants the PoolScalingController `create`, `update`, and `delete`), and `lenny-pool-config-validator`, which admits `PodSet` spec writes from the PoolScalingController's service account alone. "RV-3 Pods" of [`0140-runtime-revisions`](../0140-runtime-revisions/change.md#rv-3-pods), its §4.6.1 row, and its `component` test row say the warm pool controller keeps, creates, and deletes pod sets, while other sentences of the same section place `PodSet` writes on the PoolScalingController. The PoolScalingController does not observe the deletion conditions of RV-3 ("no session uses it", and a quarantined `AgentPod` remains, RVR-10). Options: (a) the PoolScalingController creates and deletes `PodSet` objects, and learns that a pod set holds no session and no quarantined `AgentPod` from the WarmPoolController, through a `PodSet` status condition, through the fail-closed `PodSet` deletion guard (`pkg/admission/webhook/podset_deletion_guard.go`), or through a WarmPoolController finalizer on the `PodSet`; (b) the WarmPoolController creates and deletes them, which needs an RBAC grant, an exception in `lenny-pool-config-validator`, and a rewrite of "PLC-2" **The merge.**, PLCR-4, and the §4.6.3 rows. Recommendation: (a), because the PoolScalingController already owns `spec` and the collision check of PLCR-17; the mechanism by which it learns deletability is part of the answer. Blocks: 0137.S2 and 0140.S1, and the quarantine retention that [`0128-forensics-evidence-preservation`](../0128-forensics-evidence-preservation/change.md) reads. Names 0140, which records the same conflict as its O9. (raised by the M3 readiness review, 2026-11-19; sources: 0137-F1, pod-structure-C3)
- **O6. How 0137.S2 and task 3 of 0140.S1 land relative to each other.** 0137.S2 depends on 0140.S1 and allows "0137.S2 and RV-3 land together or RV-3 first". 0140.S1's task 3 adds pod sets per pod-set key with no edge to 0137.S2, and RV-3, RV-2 **Claim.**, and RVR-16 already state the `<pool>.<key16>` names, the `lenny.dev/pod-set` label, the per-pod-set selectors, and the claim scope that PLCR-13, PLCR-17, and PLCR-18 state, so the code and its tests have two owning steps. Options: (a) 0137.S2 and 0140.S1's task 3 land on one branch, and both steps say so; (b) 0140.S1 (part) depends on 0137.S2, which then adds `podHash` and `podSetKey` itself so that no cycle forms, and RV-3 points to "PLC-2" rather than restating it; (c) 0140.S1's task 3 implements the names, labels, selectors, and claim scope that RV-3 and RV-2 state, and 0137.S2 narrows to what 0140 does not state (the PLCR-14 admission checks, the `PodSetKeyCollision` condition, the `<podSet>-warm` PDB name, and the slot fields) and lands after it. Recommendation: (a), because it keeps one owner for each test without moving fields between changes. Blocks: 0137.S2 and 0140.S1. Names 0140, which records the same question as its O10. (raised by the M3 readiness review, 2026-11-19; sources: 0137-F2, pod-structure-C4)
- **O7. Which step lands `AgentPod.spec.slotCount` and `PodSet.spec.sessionPolicy.maxConcurrentSessionsPerPod`.** 0137.S2 adds both fields and depends on [0145.S1](../0145-session-isolation/steps.md#steps) for the slot count's reader, while 0145.S1 renders the slot containers from the pool field and records the count in `spec.slotCount` (SIR-16), so the reader and the writer land before the fields exist. Neither change states where the gateway reads the recorded count for the reservation of SI-4: `agent_pod_state` gains `pod_set` (this design) and `dead_slots` (0145) but no slot count. Options: (a) 0145.S1 lands both fields and the PoolScalingController's copy of `maxConcurrentSessionsPerPod`, with [0118.S3](../0118-agent-pod-layout/steps.md#steps) for the field's name, and 0137.S2 drops the fields and its edges to 0145.S1 and 0118.S3; (b) a new 0137 step that 0145.S1 depends on lands the fields. Under either option, 0145 states the gateway's source for the recorded count, such as an `agent_pod_state` column written by the mirror or a read of the `AgentPod`. Recommendation: (a), because SI-4 is the first reader and writer. Blocks: 0137.S2 and 0145.S1. Names 0145, which records the same question as its O19, and 0118. (raised by the M3 readiness review, 2026-11-19; sources: 0137-F9, pod-structure-C5)
- **O8. Whether an earlier pod set that differs only in a tenant's assets stays claimable.** PLCR-18 claims from every `PodSet` of the session's revision's pod hash, which includes an earlier pod set of a tenant-derived runtime whose pod-set key differs only in the tenant's `inline` or `artifact` assets. "Changing the list" under "Warm stores" in [`0147-tenant-derived-runtimes`](../0147-tenant-derived-runtimes/change.md#warm-stores) keeps such pods claimable for stores, and states nothing for assets, so a pod can hold an asset that a later write replaced. Options: (a) an asset change retires the earlier pod sets from the claim set, which narrows to `PodSet` objects whose key covers the current assets; (b) they stay claimable, as for stores. Recommendation: (a), unless 0147 shows that an earlier asset can never be wrong for a new session. 0147 owns the answer and records the question as its decision 45, and this decision waits on that one. Blocks: the claim-set definition of PLCR-18 in 0137.S2. Names 0147. (raised by the M3 readiness review, 2026-11-19; sources: the "Validation owed" row of `steps.md`, program-C14)
- **O9. The change's status and whether 0137.S4 waits for the owner's cross-review.** 0137.S5 has landed, and `changes/README.md`, "Status", defines `Draft` and `Decided` as states in which no step has started and `In progress` as the state in which a step is `Building` or `Landed`. The status line still reads `Draft` because the other steps wait for the owner's cross-review ("Build of 0137.S5" in `review-history.md`), and the board holds 0137.S4, which has no dependency, as waiting on decisions for that reason alone. The program review's recommendation to set the status to `Decided` (program-C5) conflicts with the README once a step has landed. Options: (a) rule 0137.S4 ready with a `ruled-ready` line, since it fixes current defects under the current names, and set the status to `In progress` with the `change` helper when the owner records the cross-review; (b) keep 0137.S4 waiting until the cross-review ends. Recommendation: (a). Blocks: 0137.S4 and the status the board shows. (raised by the M3 readiness review, 2026-11-19; sources: 0137-F11, program-C5)
- **O10. The retention of a terminal `AgentPod`.** Nothing deletes an `AgentPod` that has reached `failed` or `terminated`, so the objects and their `agent_pod_state` rows grow with the number of pods ever created (F-4.6.32 in `BUILD-GAPS.md`, recorded on 2026-10-16 with 16,367 terminal objects against 24 warm ones; the unmerged branch finding T-6.2.16 records the same defect). §4.6.1 and §6.2 state no retention, and this change rewrites both sections without adding one. Options: (a) the AgentPod-to-Pod reconciler deletes a terminal `AgentPod` and its mirror row after a fixed grace that keeps the object readable to the occupancy projection and to an operator, with the grace as a setting; (b) the same, keeping the most recent terminal objects of each pod set up to a count; (c) leave retention to the operator and document a prune command. Recommendation: (a), with a default grace of one hour, because (b) adds a second bound to explain and (c) leaves the growth that exhausted the controller's memory limit in the e2e cluster. The rule lands in 0137.S1 and the reconciler code in 0137.S2, and both steps list F-4.6.32 as closed in part until the option is settled. The owner approved on 2026-11-19 that this change takes the finding. (raised by the M3 readiness review, 2026-11-19; sources: F-4.6.32, T-6.2.16)
- **O11. How a write to a pool setting that shapes a pod reaches warm pods.** A `PodSet.spec` carries the pool settings, and the PoolScalingController applies a write to them in place ("PLC-2" **The merge.**). A pod takes the settings that shape it when the WarmPoolController creates it: the volume sizes and the ephemeral-storage request of [PL-9](../0118-agent-pod-layout/change.md#pl-9-pool-settings-and-renames), and the `lenny.dev/egress-profile` label, which `lenny-label-immutability` holds immutable on the pod. An in-place write therefore reaches no existing pod. [RV-3](../0140-runtime-revisions/change.md#rv-3-pods) keys a pod set on the pod hash and on the derived runtime's and the pool's pod-affecting contributions, and names no pool setting among the pool's contributions. Neither this change nor the tree replaces a pod whose settings differ from its `PodSet`'s. [`0127-egress-and-git`](../0127-egress-and-git/change.md), "A pool's profile change", reports a pool's `egressProfile` as pending until no pod of the pool carries the old label, and [`0118-agent-pod-layout`](../0118-agent-pod-layout/decisions.md) asks in its O16 whether the size settings are pod-affecting contributions. Options: (a) every pool setting that the pod builder renders into the pod or its labels, including the size settings and `egressProfile`, is a pod-affecting contribution of the pool under RV-3, so a write creates a new pod set and the earlier one drains under RV-3's deletion rule; (b) the write applies in place, and the WarmPoolController replaces each idle pod whose settings differ from its `PodSet`'s and retires each busy pod at release; (c) the write applies in place and reaches pods only through the recycle and uptime limits, and the API reports it as pending until then. Recommendation: (a), because the pods of one pod set then share one specification, a write reuses RV-3's path of warming the new pod set before the old one drains, and it matches 0118's recommendation for its O16. Under (a), 0127's pending report ends when the earlier pod set's last pod leaves. Blocks: 0137.S2, 0118.S4, and the pending report of 0127. Names 0118, 0140, and 0127. (raised by the M3 readiness review, 2026-11-19; requested by changes 0118 and 0127; the matching decision is O16 of 0118, and 0127 states the pending report under "A pool's profile change" without an open decision)
