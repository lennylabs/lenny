**Gateway seam operations (0117, decision 8).** Settled by the owner on 2026-11-17 in [0117](../0117-gateway-package-layout/decisions.md), which raised the conflict between its seam interface page and this change: a member change that removes, reshapes, or adds a mechanism behind a gateway seam operation edits `spec/interfaces/gateway-seams.md` and `pkg/gateway/seam` in the step that changes the mechanism. In this change that is 0138.S1, 0138.S2, 0138.S3, and 0138.S6, whose entries in `steps.md` name the operations.

## Open decisions

The owner settled O1 to O13 on 2026-11-14 ("Decisions"), and no decision is open. The owner confirmed on 2026-11-15 the codes that revision 3 chose within O11, `INTERRUPT_UNSUPPORTED` at HTTP `409` and `INTERRUPT_TIMEOUT` at HTTP `504`, which reuses the status that the `Interrupt` RPC already returns at the deadline, and settled the `poolAttach` question of "RO-4" as S2.
