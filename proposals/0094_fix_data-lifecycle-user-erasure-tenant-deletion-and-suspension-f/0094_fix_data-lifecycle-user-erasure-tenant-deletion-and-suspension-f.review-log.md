### Traps
- **Erasure budget saga (withdrawn three times)**: rounds bounded erasure inside the shared tick with a `StartedAt + Deadline` context, then a per-tick budget (`--erasure-tick-budget`), then a DeadlineExceeded-only exemption with `fail` on `WithoutCancel`; each produced the next finding (stuck past-deadline jobs, a cancelled `store_deleting` re-walk that never completes, starvation behind Phase 4, SIGTERM turning into `failed`). Erasure is now its own unbudgeted leader-gated sweep. Do not reintroduce a run budget or a `WithoutCancel` `fail`.
- **`leaderGate.Add` for tenant deletion**: a cancelled leader context fails the in-memory tenant-deletion job (`Reconciler.fail` is terminal), so that runner keeps `IsLeader` polling. Do not unify it onto the gate.
- **Advisory locks under PgBouncer (withdrawn)**: a session-level `pg_try_advisory_lock` or `pg_advisory_lock` on a pooled connection leaks onto one backend in transaction mode. A transaction-scoped lock or SKIP LOCKED claim alone still lets a holder keep running after losing the lock. Correctness sits on `SameAttempt`; locks matter only for liveness.
- **Phase-only guard (ABA)**: a guard on `Phase` alone admits a stale executor across `fail` then `Restart` and skips the retry's re-check. `len(PhaseLog)` discriminates only while PhaseLog stays append-only; truncating it reopens the hole (T8.11's Restart case).
- **Pool-limit rules (withdrawn)**: per-tenant serialization alone, a `MaxConns` startup refusal, and a silent clamp were each tried against the salt lock's hold-and-wait; the saltlockpg root fix removed the reason.
