**Supervisor build, trust, and CH-SUPERVISE**

- **Owner decisions of 2026-10-22 on the supervisor's security review (fourth redesign).** Sender identification is retired everywhere (no `SA_SIGINFO`, no `si_pid` read, no sender property); fault signals have no handler; the implementation hazards are CODE-2 requirements with TEST-1 cases; the `setsid`/`setpgid` escape from the cleanup deadline is an accepted bound stated once in SPEC-2 **Cleanup.** Do not reopen any of these.
- **Supervisor binary.** Freestanding static C with no libc in its own digest-pinned image, staged by init container `lenny-supervisor-stage` at a dedicated UID into a volume mounted at `/lenny-supervisor`, outside `/run/lenny`; D-BINARY excludes Go for its fault-signal handlers and threads.
- **Implementation hazards (CODE-2).** SIGCHLD flag cleared before the reap loop; close-on-exec on every descriptor; `EINTR` retry on every syscall; a flag-only handler for every signal 1 to `_NSIG - 1` other than SIGKILL, SIGSTOP, and the fault signals; no `alarm` or `setitimer`; the kill-reach self-test passes only when the grandchild's `/proc` entry exists before `kill(-1)` and is gone after.
- **SPEC-6 mount rule.** No container other than `lenny-supervisor-stage` mounts the supervisor volume read-write; the volume name stays an implementation constant; `lenny-pod-security` already intercepts ephemeral containers.
- **Environment home.** SPEC-2 **Environment.**: image ENTRYPOINT, CMD, and ENV unused; fixed `PATH`; CRD `env` with the `LENNY_` prefix reserved; platform variables; set after `clearenv()`; the supervisor receives them in argv (`run --env K=V ... -- ARGV`).
- **Property self-test carrier (second redesign of 2026-10-22).** The supervisor always dials and sends `ready` first with `properties`; the adapter logs each failure and, on a `restart` pod, exits non-zero before READY when non-dumpable inspection or kill reach failed; PID-namespace init immunity is reported only (D-PROPERTIES); home SPEC-4 item 8 **Property self-test.**
- **Preflight (SPEC-13).** `lenny-ctl preflight supervisor` runs the `selftest` mode for a named RuntimeClass, with create, get, and delete on pods and get on `pods/log`; CODE-6 reads the result lines from the pod log.
- **CH-SUPERVISE authority.** `Both`, because `ready` and `exited` are unsolicited.
- **CH-SUPERVISE exclusivity.** The card's **Exclusivity.** bullet is the home: agent-pod `restartPolicy: Never` first, the adapter closes its listener after the first pre-READY accept, and the supervisor exits when the connection closes; §28.6 and §28.8 point at it; SPEC-7(h) removes the agent-pod `CrashLoopBackOff` claims at §4.7.11 item 1 step 4 and §10.3.
- **CH-SUPERVISE dial.** The supervisor repeats only a refused dial (`ECONNREFUSED`) up to `--dial-timeout` (default at least the adapter's `AwaitSupervisor` wait); an accepted connection that fails the adapter-UID check exits without a retry.
- **CH-SUPERVISE endpoint.** No manifest-nonce handshake; the reason is §4.7.11 item 8 **Why each end trusts the other.**, and the false "before any manifest exists" clause is deleted.
- **CH-SUPERVISE requests.** One outstanding request; `ping` only when no other request is outstanding; every request bounded by the deadline of the operation that sends it; an unanswered request is a link failure.
- **CH-SUPERVISE unnamed members.** The **Messages.** bullet states that the specification does not name the members of `cleanup` and `exited`, the property names, or the error codes; CODE-2 lists them as IMPLEMENTOR'S CHOICE and the TEST-1 tier-3 contract test pins them.
