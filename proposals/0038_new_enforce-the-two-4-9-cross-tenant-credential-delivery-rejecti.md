- **Scope:** A spec-to-code reconciliation plus three new enforcement surfaces for the §4.9 cross-tenant credential-delivery controls. Spec §4.9 states that the two cross-tenant-risky combinations (`deliveryMode: direct` + `isolationProfile: standard`, and `deliveryMode: proxy` + `spiffeBinding: disabled`) are blocked at two layers in multi-tenant mode, and that `lenny-preflight` fails a multi-tenant install or upgrade when a pool carries `spiffeBinding: disabled`. It reconciles the §4.9 field-locality wording in `spec/04_system-components.md` and the build-sequence bullet in `spec/18_build-sequence.md` (**SPEC-A**, **SPEC-B**), and it stages the model, admission, preflight, finalize, and test changes (**MODEL**, **LAYER1**, **PREFLIGHT**, **FINALIZE**, **TESTS**). ## 1. Problem

Spec §4.9 mandates that two cross-tenant-risky credential-delivery combinations be blocked in multi-tenant mode at two layers, and mandates a preflight scan. Layer 2 is implemented; layer 1 and the preflight scan are not; and the spec's literal field-locality wording names resources that do not carry the fields.

### (A) Layer 1 (warm-pool pool-registration validation) is unimplemented

§4.9 states the combination is "enforced at two layers: (1) the warm pool controller's pool-registration validation (rejects the admin API request before any pod is created), and (2) a `ValidatingAdmissionWebhook`" (`spec/04_system-components.md:1513`, and the parallel `spec/04:1544` for the proxy + `spiffeBinding: disabled` control). Layer 2 is implemented and unit-tested for both combinations: `RejectDirectModeStandardIsolation` (`guard.go:55`), `RejectProxyModeSpiffeBindingDisabled` (`guard.go:58`), `Decide` (`guard.go:126`), the `enforced()` gate `TenancyMode == "multi" && !DevMode` (`guard.go:162`), with a tier-5 assertion at `tests/tier5_e2e_kind/admission_credential_multitenant_test.go`. Layer 1 is absent: the only delivery-related pool-registration rejections on the warm-pool admin path are `validatePoolProxyDialect` (`credential_pools.go:167`) and the `InvalidProxyEndpointScheme` rejection (`credential_pools.go:213`), neither of which evaluates the direct/standard or proxy/spiffe combination. There is no synchronous admin-API rejection of either forbidden combination before a pod is created.

### (B) The preflight scan is unimplemented

§4.9 (`spec/04:1544`) mandates that "The `lenny-preflight` Job additionally scans all registered `CredentialPool` resources at install and upgrade time and fails when any pool in a multi-tenant deployment has `spiffeBinding: disabled`." `pkg/preflight` has no such scan: the only credential-adjacent preflight checks are the NET-064 trust-domain-uniqueness flag (`cmd/lenny-preflight/main.go:584`) and the `shareProcessNamespace` pattern (`pkg/preflight/hostsharing.go`). No preflight check reads `spiffeBinding` or `deliveryMode`.

### (C) The three credential-delivery fields live on three resources with no join

Verification uncovered the decisive data-model fact that the spec's literal wording cannot survive. The three fields the checks inspect live on three different resources:

- `isolationProfile` lives on the warm-pool admin resource (`poolstore.Pool.IsolationProfile`, `pkg/gateway/runtime/poolstore/poolstore.go:41`; `PoolPayload.IsolationProfile`, `pkg/gateway/externalapi/admin/pools.go:95`).
- `deliveryMode` and `proxyDialect`/`proxyEndpoint` live on the separate `CredentialPool` admin resource (`CredentialPoolPayload`, `pkg/gateway/externalapi/admin/credential_pools.go:24`, `deliveryMode` at `:35`). `CredentialPoolPayload` carries no `spiffeBinding` field and no reference to a warm pool.
- `deliveryMode` and `spiffeBinding` are also declared on the `SandboxTemplate` CRD (`pkg/apis/lenny/v1alpha1/sandboxtemplate_types.go:134`, `:147`), but the `PoolScalingController` never populates `SandboxTemplateSpec.DeliveryMode` or `.SpiffeBinding` when it reconciles the CRD from the poolstore source of truth (`pkg/controller/poolscaling/poolstoresource.go:65-70` copies `IsolationProfile` and `EgressProfile` and others but sets neither delivery field). Those CRD fields are dead from the source-of-truth channel, so the shipped layer-2 webhook and any preflight scan read an empty `spiffeBinding`/`deliveryMode` in the production flow.

Because `deliveryMode`/`spiffeBinding` and `isolationProfile` never coexist on the `CredentialPool` admin resource and never join to it, the spec's literal "the warm pool controller rejects any pool creation or update that sets both `deliveryMode: direct` and `isolationProfile: standard`" and "scans all registered `CredentialPool` resources for `spiffeBinding: disabled`" are not implementable as written.

### (D) The §4.9 layer-2 sentences name a resource the webhook cannot intercept

`spec/04:1513` and `spec/04:1544` both name "`SandboxTemplate` and `CredentialPool` resources" as what the `lenny-direct-mode-isolation` `ValidatingAdmissionWebhook` rejects. A `CredentialPool` is a Postgres-backed admin-API object (`spec/04` credential-pool storage; `credential_pools.go`) rather than a Kubernetes CRD, so a `ValidatingAdmissionWebhook` cannot intercept it. The shipped chart scopes the webhook to `resources: ["sandboxtemplates"]` (`charts/lenny/templates/admission-policies/direct-mode-isolation-webhook.yaml:45`), and the guard's own package doc already reasons that `SandboxTemplate` is the only admitted carrier (`guard.go:6-16`). The `CredentialPool` naming in both layer-2 sentences is contradicted by the shipped code. The preflight sentence at `:1544` is doubly wrong: `CredentialPoolPayload` has no `spiffeBinding` field, while the `SandboxTemplate` CRD carries both `deliveryMode` and `spiffeBinding`.

### (E) spec/18:343 contradicts §4.9 on the opt-in in multi-tenant mode

`spec/18_build-sequence.md:343` says the combination is rejected "unless `allowProxyModeSpiffeBindingDisabled` is set." §4.9 (`spec/04:1544`) states "The opt-in field cannot be set in multi-tenant mode — the admission webhook rejects it regardless of the field value." The `:343` clause contradicts §4.9 for multi-tenant deployments.

### (F) The gateway cannot observe tenancy.mode

A layer-1 check must gate on the platform tenancy mode. The gateway `--multi-tenant` flag defaults false (`cmd/lenny-gateway/flags.go:377`), is never wired from `.Values.tenancy.mode` in `charts/lenny/templates/gateway-deployment.yaml`, and carries an inverted auth-registry meaning (§10.2 multi-tenant claim extraction). The admin Router `Options` carry only `DevMode` (`pkg/gateway/externalapi/admin/tenants.go:404`, `:423`). No correct multi-tenant signal reaches the admin router today.

The opt-in fields `allowDirectModeStandardIsolation` and `allowProxyModeSpiffeBindingDisabled` exist only in spec prose (`spec/04:1513`, `:1544`, `spec/18:343`); they are absent from every CRD type, store, and product code path.

### (G) The delivery mode the earlier layers inspect can diverge from the delivery mode credential leasing actually uses

`deliveryMode` is authoritatively a field of the **`CredentialPool`** (`pkg/gateway/credentials/credentialpoolstore/credentialpoolstore.go:121`), and credential leasing reads it from there when it mints a lease: `assignLocked` builds the lease with `DeliveryMode: ps.pool.DeliveryMode` (`pkg/gateway/credentials/credassign/credassign.go:324`), and that value is what decides whether the real key is materialized into the pod (`direct`) or kept on the gateway behind the proxy (`proxy`). The warm-pool/`SandboxTemplate` `deliveryMode` that layers 1 and 2 inspect (the field MODEL populates) is a **separate, denormalized copy**: `poolstore.Pool` carries no `CredentialPool` reference, and the credential/provider pools a session uses are resolved independently at session finalize from the tenant `credentialPolicy.providerPools` intersected with the runtime `supportedProviders` (`resolveCredentialPools`, `pkg/gateway/sessionserver/start.go:1281`, intersection at `:1301`). A session can hold multiple leases — one per provider (`spec/04:1248`) — whose pools may declare different delivery modes, so there is no single warm-pool delivery mode that faithfully represents them. If a warm pool/`SandboxTemplate` declares `deliveryMode: proxy` (so layers 1 and 2 admit it with `isolationProfile: standard`) while the `CredentialPool` a session resolves at finalize declares `deliveryMode: direct`, leasing materializes the real key into a `standard`-isolation (shared-kernel) pod in multi-tenant mode — the exact cross-tenant exposure the control exists to prevent, admitted because the registration and admission layers checked a copy rather than the delivery mode leasing uses. The registration and admission layers are still the correct fail-fast controls on the declared configuration; the divergence is closed by evaluating the effective per-provider delivery mode at every point a session's credential pools are resolved and its leases are minted, which spans several session-start seams rather than the `/finalize` step alone.

## 2. Decisions

- **Layer 1 enforces on the warm-pool admin registration path rather than the CredentialPool admin API.** §4.9 names the warm-pool controller as the layer-1 enforcer, and `isolationProfile` already lives on the warm-pool admin resource (`poolstore.Pool`, `PoolPayload`). The `CredentialPool` admin resource carries neither `isolationProfile` nor `spiffeBinding` and has no reference to a warm pool, so it can never observe either forbidden combination; placing layer 1 there would force duplicating `isolationProfile`/`spiffeBinding` onto an unrelated resource. The warm-pool admin create/update handlers (`handleCreatePool`, `pools.go:491`; `handleUpdatePool`) are the seam, alongside the existing tenancy/acknowledgment gates there (`AllowStandardIsolation` at `validatePoolForStore`, `pools.go:408`; `AcknowledgeNonceOnlyAuth`).
- **Make the SandboxTemplate deliveryMode/spiffeBinding fields live by carrying them onto the warm-pool admin/store model.** `deliveryMode` and `spiffeBinding` are declared on the `SandboxTemplate` CRD and inspected by the shipped layer-2 webhook, but `PoolScalingController.toConfig` (`poolstoresource.go:65-70`) never populates them, so they are dead from the Postgres source of truth. Add `DeliveryMode` and `SpiffeBinding` (plus the two opt-in bools) to `poolstore.Pool` and `PoolPayload`/`UpdatePoolRequest`, and populate `SandboxTemplateSpec.DeliveryMode`/`.SpiffeBinding` in `toConfig`. One admin resource then carries the whole combination for layer 1, and the shipped layer-2 webhook and the new preflight scan read live values in the production flow, closing a latent hole in layer 2. This applies option (b) to the warm pool (the resource that already carries `isolationProfile`) rather than to the `CredentialPool`.
- **Reuse the shipped direct_mode_isolation.Decide as the single canonical rule for all enforcement points.** Layer 1 and the preflight scan build a `direct_mode_isolation.Request{TenancyMode, DevMode, Kind, DeliveryMode, IsolationProfile, SpiffeBinding, EgressProfile}` and call `Decide`, which already returns the exact spec error codes (`guard.go:55`, `:58`, `:143-153`) and applies the exact `enforced()` predicate `TenancyMode == "multi" && !DevMode` (`guard.go:162`). `EgressProfile` is carried so layer 1 and preflight also reject the NET-006 `proxy` + `provider-direct` combination `Decide` denies in every tenancy mode (`guard.go:132-138`), keeping the earlier layers a superset of the layer-2 gate rather than admitting a pool the webhook would later reject on the reconciled `SandboxTemplate`. No second copy of the rule is written; the webhook, the warm-pool admin, and preflight share one decision function. `Decide`'s `Request` struct carries no opt-in fields, so the rule stays canonical rather than being wrapped in bespoke opt-in branches.
- **Thread tenancy.mode into the gateway via a new --tenancy-mode flag.** The flag mirrors the webhook binary's existing `--tenancy-mode` flag (`charts/lenny/templates/admission-policies/_webhook.tpl:72`) rather than overloading the semantically inverted `--multi-tenant` auth flag. Wire it from `.Values.tenancy.mode` in `gateway-deployment.yaml`, add `TenancyMode` to admin `Options` and the Router, and gate layer 1's `enforced()` on it. This reuses the webhook's flag name and the guard's predicate and fixes the confirmed gap that the gateway currently has no correct multi-tenant signal.
- **Honor the opt-in fields exactly as §4.9 states, and no more.** In multi-tenant mode `Decide` rejects both forbidden combinations regardless of any opt-in value (`guard.go:124-153`), which is the correct reading of "the webhook rejects it regardless of the field value." Layer 1 therefore rejects through `Decide` and adds no bespoke branch keyed on the opt-in bool being set. In single-tenant or development mode `Decide` allows the combination and layer 1 emits a warning event. The existing `maybeEmitWeakIsolation` helper (`pools.go:648`) emits the `pool.direct_mode_weak_isolation` warning only, and returns early unless the pool is `standard`-runc isolation with `allowStandardIsolation: true` (`pools.go:649-651`), so it never fires for a `proxy` + `spiffeBinding: disabled` pool. Layer 1 therefore keeps `maybeEmitWeakIsolation` for the `direct` + `standard` warning and adds a sibling emitter for a new `pool.proxy_mode_spiffe_binding_disabled` warning event keyed on `SpiffeBinding == "disabled"` with `DeliveryMode == "proxy"`, which spec/04:1544 requires be emitted at disablement. Layer 1 does not add a hard rejection when the opt-in field is absent in single-tenant or dev mode: §4.9 states only that the combination "is permitted with an explicit opt-in field on the pool ... and emits a `DirectModeWeakIsolation` warning event," and does not state the absent-opt-in behavior. The reject-on-absent question was raised as an open decision and **resolved by the reviewer as permit-with-warning** (§9): the proposal implements the literal §4.9 behavior (allow with the warning event in single-tenant/dev mode), and the stricter reject-on-absent posture is not adopted because it would require a prior §4.9 spec edit rather than an implementation-time invention. The two opt-in bools are carried on the model as the spec-named deployer acknowledgment fields and surfaced on GET; enforcement of their absence is deferred.
- **One unified preflight check covers whatever Decide rejects.** The check lists `SandboxTemplate` resources (the model is `gatherPoolGracePeriods`, `pkg/preflight/terminationgrace.go:70`, which already lists `lennyv1.SandboxTemplateList` and is RBAC-provisioned in the preflight Job), builds a `Request` per template, and calls `Decide`; any rejection fails the check fail-closed with a `Decision{Passed: false, Reason}` carrying the guard's error code, matching `pkg/preflight/hostsharing.go`. Because `Decide` evaluates both combinations, the check covers `direct` + `standard` as an incidental consequence of reusing the canonical rule; the spec's normative preflight mandate stays scoped to `proxy` + `spiffeBinding: disabled` (SPEC-B does not add a `direct` + `standard` install-time rejection, to avoid a new §4.9-internal inconsistency between the two-layer `:1513` block and the `:1544` preflight sentence). The scan is gated on `tenancy.mode: multi` passed as a preflight flag, reusing the existing `--playground-multi-tenant={{ eq .Values.tenancy.mode "multi" }}` and `--dev-mode` chart precedent (`charts/lenny/templates/preflight-job.yaml:382`, `:413`).
- **Reconcile the §4.9 field-locality wording narrowly.** The §4.9 layer-2 sentences at `:1513` and `:1544` name `CredentialPool` resources as carriers the webhook rejects; correct them to name `SandboxTemplate` resources only, with a justifying clause that a `CredentialPool` is a Postgres-backed admin object the webhook cannot intercept. Retarget the `:1544` preflight sentence to scan `SandboxTemplate` pool definitions for `deliveryMode: proxy` + `spiffeBinding: disabled`, keeping it scoped to its current single mandate. Correct `spec/18:343` so the `allowProxyModeSpiffeBindingDisabled` opt-in permits the combination only in single-tenant or development mode.
- **Add a credential-delivery admission gate on the effective per-provider delivery mode at every session-start mint seam, reusing the same `Decide` (FINALIZE).** The registration and admission layers inspect the warm-pool/`SandboxTemplate` `deliveryMode`, which §1(G) shows is a denormalized copy that can diverge from the `CredentialPool` `deliveryMode` credential leasing resolves per session. The effective delivery mode is concrete once a session's credential pools are resolved (`resolveCredentialPools`, `start.go:1281`) and its pod match is known, so the gate runs at that point rather than at pool registration (where the `CredentialPool` a session will use is not yet known). A session mints credential leases on several seams, and the gate must run on each of them before the mint: the two-step finalize path (`prepareAtFinalize`, `finalize.go:208`, before `podBinder.Prepare`, `finalize.go:268`); the combined one-call `POST /v1/sessions/start` path (`handleCreateAndStart` → `startOnPod`, `start.go:736`, `:1691`, which reaches `prepareAndLaunch` → `podBinder.Prepare`, `start.go:1775`, `:2011`); the resume/tree-recovery rebuild path (`resumeOnPod` → `startOnPod` with `claimed == nil`, `start.go:3092`, which reaches the whole-sequence `podBinder.Bind`, `start.go:1785`); and the concurrent-workspace slot path (`match.MaxConcurrentSessions > 1`), which `prepareAtFinalize` skips (`finalize.go:238`) and which mints per-provider leases through `bindConcurrentSlot` → `BindReservedSlot` (reached from `startOnPod` at `start.go:1746` and from `launchOnPod` at `start.go:1864`). The gate is factored into one shared helper that builds a `direct_mode_isolation.Request` for each resolved provider pool with `DeliveryMode` read from that pool's `CredentialPool` record (`byName[poolName].DeliveryMode`, `start.go:1307`), `IsolationProfile` from `match.IsolationProfile` (`resolve.go:94`), and `SpiffeBinding` from `match.SpiffeBinding`, and calls `Decide`; the helper is invoked at each seam above after the pools and pod match are resolved and before the lease mint. On `!Allowed` in multi-tenant mode the session is rejected with a 422 carrying the guard's error code and `Decision.Reason`, before any credential is minted or pushed. On the `prepareAtFinalize` seam the gate reclaims the create-time pod (`reclaimFinalizedPod`) before returning the rejection, matching the two existing pre-Prepare exits there (`finalize.go:231`, `:251`), because the outer finalize handler does not reclaim on a pre-Prepare error (`sessionserver.go:2941-2943`); on the combined and resume seams the typed rejection is a pre-bind error the existing `startOnPod` rollback path already releases (`createClaimNeedsRollback` → `rollbackClaim`, `start.go:2443`, `:744`), the same treatment a `ResolvePool` failure receives. This closes the §1(G) divergence by evaluating the delivery mode leasing uses. It is a defense-in-depth backstop, and layers 1–3 still fail fast on the declared configuration. `SpiffeBinding` is a warm-pod/`SandboxTemplate` property absent from the `CredentialPool` record, so the gate pairs the effective `CredentialPool` delivery mode with the bound pod's `spiffeBinding`/`isolationProfile`; it needs `SpiffeBinding` added to `PoolMatch` (sourced from `tmpl.Spec.SpiffeBinding`, `resolve.go:283`) and a string `TenancyMode` threaded into the sessionserver `Options` from the same `--tenancy-mode` flag LAYER1 adds, because the sessionserver carries only the semantically-inverted `MultiTenant`/`DevMode` bools today (`sessionserver.go:1087`, `:1076`) and not a correct tenancy signal.
- **Classified new.** The dominant action adds three enforcement surfaces the implementation lacks (the warm-pool admin layer-1 check, the preflight scan, and the session-start credential-delivery gate) and four new model fields, with a bounded spec reconciliation of field-locality wording and one companion §4.9 sentence for the gate. All error codes and messages come from the already-shipped guard, cited as durable spec sections (`// spec: §4.9`).

## 3. How the enforcement points fit and how the fields flow

A deployer registers a warm pool through the admin API. The `PoolPayload` now carries `isolationProfile`, `deliveryMode`, `spiffeBinding`, and the two opt-in bools, so a single admin resource holds the whole credential-delivery combination. `handleCreatePool` builds a `direct_mode_isolation.Request` from the pool built by `poolFromPayload` plus the gateway's `TenancyMode`/`DevMode` and calls `Decide` (**layer 1**); the update path evaluates the merged effective pool (the stored row merged with the patch's non-nil fields, because a `PUT` is a partial patch) in the shared `validatePoolUpdate` before either the dry-run preview or the persisted write. In multi-tenant mode a forbidden combination is rejected with a 422 carrying the guard's error code before the pool is stored, so no `SandboxTemplate` CRD is ever reconciled and no pod is created. In single-tenant or dev mode `Decide` allows the pool and layer 1 emits the weak-isolation warning event.

The `§17.6 bootstrap.pools` seed list is the second gateway pool-admission write path. `upsertPools` (`pkg/gateway/externalapi/admin/bootstrap_resources.go:27`) accepts `[]PoolPayload`, builds each pool via `poolFromPayload`, and writes it with `r.pools.Create`/`r.pools.Update` (`:85`, `:107`). Its own doc comment states it holds each seed entry to the same fail-closed gate as the `POST /v1/admin/pools` handler (`:73-77`). Layer 1 therefore also runs on the seed path: `upsertPools` builds the same `direct_mode_isolation.Request` from the seed pool plus `r.tenancyMode`/`r.devMode` and calls `Decide` before either store write, and a forbidden combination is recorded as a per-entry seed validation error rather than being stored. Without this the seed path would store a `direct` + `standard`, `proxy` + `spiffeBinding: disabled`, or `proxy` + `provider-direct` pool in multi-tenant mode, which reconciles into a `SandboxTemplate` the `failurePolicy: Fail` layer-2 webhook then rejects at CRD apply, wedging the `PoolScalingController` reconcile permanently.

If a pool is stored, `PoolScalingController.toConfig` reconciles the `SandboxTemplate` CRD from the poolstore and now populates `spec.DeliveryMode` and `spec.SpiffeBinding` alongside `spec.IsolationProfile`. The **layer-2** `lenny-direct-mode-isolation` `ValidatingAdmissionWebhook`, which inspects those CRD fields, therefore reads live values rather than the empty strings it reads today. Layer 2 is the fail-closed backstop on CRD apply.

At install and upgrade time the `lenny-preflight` Job lists `SandboxTemplate` resources and calls `Decide` on each (**layer 3**, the preflight scan), failing the multi-tenant install fail-closed when any pool carries the `proxy` + `spiffeBinding: disabled` combination the spec names (and, incidentally, the `direct` + `standard` and `proxy` + `provider-direct` combinations the same canonical `Decide` rejects).

Layers 1–3 all inspect the pool-definition `deliveryMode` (the warm-pool/`SandboxTemplate` copy). When a session starts, the effective delivery mode becomes concrete: the gateway resolves the session's credential pools from the tenant `credentialPolicy.providerPools` intersected with the runtime `supportedProviders` (`resolveCredentialPools`, `start.go:1281`), and each resolved `CredentialPool` carries its own authoritative `deliveryMode`. The **credential-delivery gate** builds a `direct_mode_isolation.Request` for each resolved provider pool with `DeliveryMode` from that `CredentialPool` record, `IsolationProfile`/`SpiffeBinding` from the bound pod's `PoolMatch`, and calls the same `Decide`, rejecting the session in multi-tenant mode before any lease is minted. Because a session mints leases on more than one seam, the gate runs on each of them: the two-step finalize (`prepareAtFinalize`, before `podBinder.Prepare` at `finalize.go:268`), the combined one-call `POST /v1/sessions/start` (`startOnPod` → `prepareAndLaunch`, `start.go:1775`), the resume/tree-recovery rebuild (`startOnPod` → the whole-sequence `podBinder.Bind`, `start.go:1785`), and the concurrent-workspace slot path (`bindConcurrentSlot` → `BindReservedSlot`, reached from both `startOnPod` and `launchOnPod`), which `prepareAtFinalize` skips (`finalize.go:238`). This closes the §1(G) case where a pool-definition `deliveryMode` layers 1–3 admitted differs from the `CredentialPool` `deliveryMode` leasing actually uses. It is a defense-in-depth backstop, and the fail-fast registration and admission checks remain in force.

The gateway learns its tenancy mode from the new `--tenancy-mode` flag wired from `.Values.tenancy.mode`, the same value the webhook binary and the preflight Job already read, threaded into both the admin Router and the sessionserver `Options`. All the enforcement points evaluate the identical `Decide` predicate keyed off the identical `tenancy.mode`, so a combination that layer 1 rejects at registration is the same combination layers 2, 3, and the credential-delivery gate would reject. Because `handleCreatePool`/`handleUpdatePool` pass `EgressProfile` into the `Request`, layer 1 also rejects the NET-006 `proxy` + `provider-direct` combination `Decide` denies in every tenancy mode, which keeps layer 1 a proper superset of the layer-2 gate and prevents a stored pool from reconciling into a `SandboxTemplate` the `failurePolicy: Fail` webhook would then reject, wedging the `PoolScalingController`.

## 4. Proposed changes

### SPEC-A. Reconcile §4.9 field-locality wording for the direct + standard rejection

```go
func (s *Server) checkCredentialDeliveryIsolation(match podsession.PoolMatch, poolDeliveryModes map[string]string) error {
    for _, mode := range poolDeliveryModes {
        dec := direct_mode_isolation.Decide(direct_mode_isolation.Request{
            TenancyMode:      s.tenancyMode,          // threaded from --tenancy-mode
            DevMode:          s.devMode,              // existing sessionserver field
            Kind:             "Session",
            DeliveryMode:     mode,                   // the resolved CredentialPool's effective mode
            IsolationProfile: string(match.IsolationProfile),
            SpiffeBinding:    match.SpiffeBinding,
        })
        if !dec.Allowed {
            return /* typed rejection carrying dec.Code / dec.Reason */
        }
    }
    return nil
}
```

## 5. Non-goals

- **Collapsing the two `deliveryMode` fields into a single source of truth.** The credential-leasing subsystem reads `deliveryMode` from the `CredentialPool` (`pkg/gateway/credentials/credassign/credassign.go:324`), while the pool-registration and admission layers read the warm-pool/`SandboxTemplate` copy. This proposal does not remove either field or add a warm-pool→`CredentialPool` reference; a warm pod can serve multiple providers with different delivery modes (`spec/04:1248`), so there is no single warm-pool delivery mode to unify to. Instead of eliminating the divergence at the data-model level, FINALIZE closes it at the enforcement level by evaluating the effective per-provider `CredentialPool` `deliveryMode` at session finalize (§1(G)). A full data-model unification (dropping the denormalized field and resolving one effective delivery mode per warm pool) is a larger §4.9 redesign left out of scope. This proposal also does not change how credential leasing selects proxy versus direct.
- **Changing the layer-2 webhook decision logic.** `guard.go` is reused verbatim as the single canonical rule; no new rejection codes, request fields, or gating are added to it.
- **Repurposing or removing the `--multi-tenant` auth flag or its permissive-registry behavior.** A separate `--tenancy-mode` flag is added instead.
- **Adding the two opt-in bool fields to the SandboxTemplate CRD.** Layer 2 ignores them by design (rejects regardless in multi-tenant mode), so they live only on the warm-pool admin/store model that layer 1 reads.
- **Adding a hard single-tenant rejection when the opt-in is absent, or a multi-tenant rejection keyed on the opt-in bool being set.** Both overshoot §4.9. The single-tenant permit-with-warning behavior is implemented and is the reviewer-ratified resolution (§9); a stricter reject-on-absent posture would require a prior §4.9 spec edit and is not adopted here.
- **A separately-flagged `direct` + `standard` install-time preflight mandate in the spec.** The PREFLIGHT code covers `direct` + `standard` incidentally because `Decide` rejects it, but SPEC-B does not add a normative `direct` + `standard` preflight requirement, to avoid a `:1513`-vs-`:1544` inconsistency.
- **Emitting the `credential.proxy_mode_spiffe_binding_disabled` / `DirectModeWeakIsolation` audit-event lifecycle beyond the pool-registration warning event.** The single-tenant/dev pool-registration warning events are in scope and emitted (the `pool.direct_mode_weak_isolation` event through `maybeEmitWeakIsolation` and the `pool.proxy_mode_spiffe_binding_disabled` event through the new sibling emitter, satisfying the spec/04:1544 requirement that disablement emit a warning event). The broader `credential.*` audit-stream lifecycle for these names (`pkg/credential/auditevents.go:81`) stays out of scope for the registration gate.

## 9. Open decisions for review

Both prior open decisions are resolved in this revision:

- **Delivery-mode divergence between subsystems — resolved (add an enforcement-time gate rather than a data-model unification).** The pool-registration and admission layers read the warm-pool/`SandboxTemplate` `deliveryMode` copy, while credential leasing reads the authoritative per-provider `CredentialPool` `deliveryMode` (`credassign.go:324`). Rather than unify the two fields (which is ill-defined because a warm pod can serve multiple providers with different delivery modes, `spec/04:1248`), the reviewer chose to close the divergence at the enforcement level: FINALIZE adds a credential-delivery admission gate that evaluates the effective per-provider `CredentialPool` `deliveryMode` against the bound pod's `isolationProfile`/`spiffeBinding` and rejects in multi-tenant mode before any lease is minted, run through one shared helper at every session-start mint seam (finalize, the combined one-call start, resume/tree-recovery rebuild, and the concurrent-workspace slot path) so no mint path bypasses it (§1(G), §2 FINALIZE, SPEC-C). A full data-model unification (dropping the denormalized field, resolving one effective delivery mode per warm pool) is recorded as an out-of-scope §4.9 redesign in Non-goals.
- **Absent-opt-in behavior in single-tenant/dev mode — resolved (permit with warning).** The reviewer ratified the literal §4.9 behavior: in single-tenant or development mode a forbidden combination without the opt-in is permitted and emits the warning event; it is not hard-rejected. §4.9 states only "permitted with an explicit opt-in" and does not mandate reject-on-absent, so the stricter acknowledgment-gate posture (mirroring `AllowStandardIsolation` at `validatePoolForStore:408`) is not adopted; it would require a prior §4.9 spec edit. The two opt-in bools remain on the model and surface on GET.
