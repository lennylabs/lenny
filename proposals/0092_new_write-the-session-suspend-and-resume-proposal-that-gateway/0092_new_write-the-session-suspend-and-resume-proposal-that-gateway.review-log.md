### Traps

**Do not restore the old SPEC-1b condition, `UpdatedAt` as guard or token, the post-exit bump, or the deleted SPEC-1b claims.** State plus generation is ABA-prone; `AdvanceLastSeq` moves `UpdatedAt`.
**Terminal writers read `fromState` outside their mutator.** `handleTransition` and `handleDelete` read it on the locked row (CODE-6 item 1); other terminal writers remain a §7.2 step 4 defect owned elsewhere.
**Binding stamp.** `resume_rebind.go` stamped `G−1`; CODE-4 item 5.4 restamps with `G`.
**Do not add a `resuming` guard to §10.1.2 step 1.** SPEC-2a's step-0 tie and SPEC-1b exits carry exclusivity; the step-1 0-row path covers the race order.
**Fence-restatement sweeps.** Grep with lines joined; "must have received" hides from "must receive". No §28 site may describe how a podless session acquires the holder role; §28.6 **One holder per session.** is the home, and its "`CH-FENCE` establishes the constraint" stays.
**"No bound pod" is read from state.** Recovering rows name the failed pod; fixtures must carry it.
**Do not describe a pointer's target in SPEC-1f.**
**Replaced: inbox buffering, off-holder forward, `DrainForResume`, `clearInboxOnResume`, drain after commit.** Each lost to duplicate or stranded delivery; do not restore retract-after-drain, expiry in the delivery loop, the DLQ sweep of `suspended` rows, the durable inbox `EXPIRE`, or a backlog resume keyed on an interrupt suspension's inbox.
**Release migration is not the forbidden entry migration.** The trap forbids migrating on entry to `suspended`; the release migrates after the clear. Do not move it to the `running → suspended` write.
**Replaced: migrate before the clear.** A held-pod resume's `deliverBuffered` mid-`Send` would deliver twice. Clear first.
**Accepted migration windows.** A crash loses the in-memory inbox, and a failed post-migration re-read on a terminal session leaves migrated entries with no reclaimer (SPEC-5z item 11). Refile only with a widening path.
**No at-least-once sentence in SPEC-5i.** Do not cite `delivered_message_ids`.
**Never route a refused held entry by path 6 or re-enqueue it.**
