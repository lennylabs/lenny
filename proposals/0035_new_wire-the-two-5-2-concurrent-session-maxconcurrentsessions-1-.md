# Proposal: Wire the two §5.2 concurrent-session (`maxConcurrentSessions > 1`) recycle reports that proposal 0031 spun out: the last-slot occupancy-zero whole-pod scrub trigger through `Binder.ReleaseSlot`, and the per-slot `ReportSessionScrub` cleanup report, both implementation-only against the committed §5.2 contract and 0031's landed adapter seam

- **Status:** Verified (2026-07-02). Converged after 10 adversarial review rounds (11 findings fixed); awaiting sign-off.
- **Date:** 2026-07-03.
- **Scope:** An implementation-only change against the already-committed §5.2 scrub contract and proposal 0031's landed adapter seam. It wires the two adapter-side reports a recycling concurrent-session pool (`sessionPolicy.maxConcurrentSessions > 1` with `recycle.enabled: true`) owes but never sends today. **(A)** the whole-pod scrub trigger on the concurrent last-slot occupancy-zero boundary, which reaches the §5.2 whole-pod scrub through `Binder.ReleaseSlot` rather than the session-mode `Binder.Release` 0031 wired (**C-A1** reorders the gateway release so the recycle `Shutdown` fires after the claim is patched to `recycling`, **C-A2** runs the scrub on the adapter outside the early-returning slot branch); and **(B)** the per-slot cleanup report `ReportSessionScrub(released|leaked)` on every concurrent slot release, bounded by the per-slot cleanup timeout (**C-B1**), plus the gateway `ReportSessionScrub` consumer driving the `slotstate` registry and the `lenny_adapter_leaked_slots` gauge from the reported outcome (**C-B2**). A supporting change threads the pool's scrub configuration onto the concurrent bind result, delivers the pod identity to the adapter through the controller pod-spec builder, and delivers the per-slot cleanup budget through the gateway slot-bind RPC (**C-W1**), and the tests span tiers 1, 2, 3, 4, 7, and 9 (**C-T**). It touches `pkg/gateway/podlifecycle/podclaim/slotclaimer.go`, `pkg/gateway/podlifecycle/podsession/slotbinder.go`, `pkg/gateway/session/executor/pod.go`, `pkg/gateway/sessionserver/start.go`, `pkg/adapter/session.go`, `pkg/adapter/slotsession.go`, `pkg/adapter/slot.go`, `pkg/adapter/server.go` / `pkg/adapter/gatewaylink.go`, `pkg/gateway/podlifecycle/podsession/resolve.go`, `pkg/gateway/podlifecycle/podsession/binder.go`, `pkg/gateway/mcpfabric/delegationtree/leasecontrol/scrubreport_server.go`, `pkg/controller/sandbox/podspec`, `schemas/lenny-adapter.proto` and its generated Go (the adapter `StartSessionRequest`), `pkg/gateway/runtime/adapterclient/client.go`, `cmd/lenny-adapter`, and the tests. It stages **no** spec change: §5.2 mandates the whole-pod scrub uniformly across session-mode configurations including `maxConcurrentSessions > 1` (`spec/05_runtime-registry-and-pool-model.md:451,:457`) and the per-slot `ReportSessionScrub` with its `max(cleanupTimeoutSeconds / maxConcurrentSessions, 5)` timeout (`spec/05:541`), and §6.2 counts leaked slots toward the unhealthy-replacement threshold and the `lenny_adapter_leaked_slots` gauge (`spec/06_warm-pod-model.md:151`). Proposal 0031 (Approved 2026-07-02) is a hard prerequisite: it lands the `RecycleScrub` sub-message, the adapter `startPodScrub` driver, the `adapterclient.ShutdownRecycle` variant, the `ReportPodScrub` production caller, and the retained `*gatewaycontrol.Client` on the adapter `Server`, which both boundaries plug into. The sequencing mirrors proposal 0034, which is likewise a 0031 follow-on. Until this lands, a concurrent recycling pod at occupancy zero is retired fail-closed by the gateway missing-report timeout, and leaked slots go unreported to the gateway ledger; both are availability and efficiency gaps rather than isolation breaches.

This document stages the proposed code and test changes. It does not modify any spec, code, or doc file. Apply the changes in the "Proposed changes" section after sign-off.

## 1. Problem

§5.2 mandates two adapter-side reports for a recycling concurrent-session pool. A pool with `sessionPolicy.maxConcurrentSessions > 1` and `recycle.enabled: true` is a valid configuration the pool controller admits: it rejects `recycle.allowCrossTenantReuse` on such a pool (`pkg/gateway/runtime/poolstore/poolstore.go:536-539`) while permitting recycling itself. Neither report has a production caller today, and proposal 0031 deliberately spun both out as implementation-only follow-ons (0031 Non-goals).

### (A) The concurrent occupancy-zero whole-pod scrub trigger

§5.2 states the whole-pod scrub "runs whenever occupancy reaches zero on a recycling pod before the pod is reused, reported via `ReportPodScrub`," uniformly across session-mode configurations including `maxConcurrentSessions > 1` (`spec/05_runtime-registry-and-pool-model.md:451,:457`). The concurrent last-slot drain reaches that boundary through a different release path than session-mode `Binder.Release`. `Binder.ReleaseSlot` sends only a per-slot `ShutdownSlot(sessionID, slotID)` and immediately closes the adapter connection (`pkg/gateway/podlifecycle/podsession/slotbinder.go:494-500`), then delegates to `SlotClaimer.ReleaseSlot`, which on occupancy zero patches the per-pod claim `bound → recycling` (`WriteRecyclingStatus`) and arms the gateway missing-report timeout (`RecycleBoundary.OnRecycling`) but triggers no adapter scrub and returns only an `error` (`pkg/gateway/podlifecycle/podclaim/slotclaimer.go:679-704`). The comment at `slotclaimer.go:684-685` asserts "The adapter runs the whole-pod scrub on occupancy zero," but nothing wires it. The adapter's `Shutdown` handler early-returns into `shutdownSlot` on any slot-qualified request (`pkg/adapter/session.go:226-227`) before any whole-pod logic. A concurrent recycling pod at occupancy zero is therefore always retired fail-closed by the missing-report timeout and never reused. The gap is availability and efficiency rather than an isolation breach: the pod is terminated and replaced, the stronger default posture.

### (B) The per-slot cleanup report `ReportSessionScrub`

§5.2 mandates that on every slot completion or failure the adapter removes the slot workspace, kills the slot process group, releases the `slotId`, and reports each slot's cleanup outcome (`released` or `leaked`) to the gateway via `ReportSessionScrub`, with the per-slot cleanup timeout `max(cleanupTimeoutSeconds / maxConcurrentSessions, 5)` seconds and a 5s floor enforced at runtime by the adapter (`spec/05:541`). The adapter emitter `Client.ReportSessionScrub` (`pkg/adapter/gatewaycontrol/scrubreport.go:80`) and the gateway handler with its full ledger both exist: the handler routes `ReportSessionScrub` → `RecordSessionScrub` → `IncrementSessionsServed` plus `RecordLeak` into the unhealthy-threshold drain ledger (`pkg/gateway/mcpfabric/delegationtree/leasecontrol/scrubreport_server.go:75,:389-405`). The adapter has zero production callers of the emitter (only `*_test.go`). The adapter slot-teardown path (`shutdownSlot` / `releaseSlot` → `removeSlotTree`, `pkg/adapter/slotsession.go:63-104`, `pkg/adapter/slot.go:133`) performs the cleanup without reporting `released` or `leaked` and without the per-slot cleanup timeout. Consequently, on the concurrent per-slot cleanup path a leaked slot is never reported to the gateway at all today. §5.2:541 and §6.2:151 require a leaked slot to produce three distinct gateway effects: the `ceil(maxConcurrentSessions/2)` "fail or leak" unhealthy-replacement threshold (the drain ledger), the `lenny_adapter_leaked_slots` gauge (published by the gateway from its `slotstate.Registry`, `spec/06_warm-pod-model.md:151`), and continued occupancy in the pod's Redis slot counter that prevents over-assignment. The gateway's `ReportSessionScrub` consumer today drives only the first of the three (`RecordSessionScrub` → `IncrementSessionsServed` plus `RecordLeak`, `scrubreport_server.go:389-405`): it discards the `slotId` and never touches the `slotstate.Registry` or the gauge, which are driven only by the slot-bind-failure retry path (`start.go:2144-2151`).

### Implementation-only, sequenced after 0031

This change is implementation-only against the committed spec. 0031's S-A1 defines the recycle-scrub trigger contract "for every occupancy-zero recycle boundary," and §5.2 (`spec/05:451,:457,:541`) already mandates both reports. It has a hard prerequisite: proposal 0031 must be implemented first, because it lands the seam both boundaries plug into. Verified unimplemented today: `scrub.Run` and `Client.ReportPodScrub` have zero production callers, `RecycleScrub` is absent from `schemas/lenny-adapter.proto`, `pkg/adapter/podscrub.go` does not exist, and no `ShutdownRecycle` symbol exists. This is the concurrent-session (`maxConcurrentSessions > 1`) occupancy-zero whole-pod scrub follow-on and the per-slot `ReportSessionScrub` follow-on that 0031's Non-goals and §7 filed as separate implementation-only findings against the §5.2 contract S-A1 defines. The sequencing mirrors proposal 0034, which is likewise a 0031 follow-on gated on the same seam.

## 2. Decisions

- **0031 is a hard prerequisite and this change is sequenced after it, exactly as proposal 0034 is.** 0031 lands the seam both boundaries plug into: the `RecycleScrub` sub-message on `ShutdownRequest` (0031 C-A1), the adapter `startPodScrub` driver that runs `scrub.Run` and emits `ReportPodScrub` (0031 C-A2), the `adapterclient.ShutdownRecycle` variant (0031 C-A3), and the retained `*gatewaycontrol.Client` on the adapter `Server` as a `PodScrubReporter`. All are verified absent today. There is no seam to plug into otherwise.
- **No spec prose change is required.** §5.2 mandates the whole-pod scrub uniformly across session-mode configurations including `maxConcurrentSessions > 1` (`spec/05:451,:457`), and 0031's S-A1 writes the trigger contract for every occupancy-zero recycle boundary. §5.2:541 mandates `ReportSessionScrub` per slot with the `max(cleanupTimeoutSeconds / maxConcurrentSessions, 5)` timeout, and §6.2:151 mandates that leaked slots feed the unhealthy threshold and the gauge. Both (A) and (B) wire behavior the committed spec already requires. This is classified `new` because it adds a capability the implementation lacks (concurrent recycle reuse and per-slot leak reporting), consistent with how 0031 and 0034 classify their implementation-only follow-ons.
- **Reuse the existing `Shutdown` RPC and 0031's `RecycleScrub` sub-message rather than adding a parallel RPC.** (A) reuses the whole-pod recycle `Shutdown` (`ShutdownRecycle`) 0031 built for session-mode. (B) needs the adapter to learn the pod identity and the per-slot cleanup budget on every slot release; deliver them as pod-lifetime constants at pod creation (see C-W1) rather than a new RPC or a second control stream.
- **The whole-pod-scrub consumer side is built and is not touched; the per-slot `ReportSessionScrub` consumer needs the registry and gauge wiring.** `RecordPodScrub` / `podscrub.Decide`, the recycle-boundary missing-report timeout, and their wiring already consume `ReportPodScrub` and drive recycle-versus-retire. `RecordSessionScrub` already drives `IncrementSessionsServed` and the `RecordLeak` drain ledger (the `ceil(maxConcurrentSessions/2)` threshold, `scrubreport_server.go:389-405`), but it discards the `slotId` and never touches the `slotstate.Registry` or the `lenny_adapter_leaked_slots` gauge, which today are driven only by the slot-bind-failure retry path (`start.go:2144-2151`). C-B2 extends `RecordSessionScrub` to drive the registry and the gauge from the reported outcome. The bind-failure path's registry is created privately inside the sessionserver (`slotStates: slotstate.NewRegistry()` at `sessionserver.go:1701`, not injected from `Options`), and the `ScrubReporter` is built on a separate composition path whose `ScrubReporterOptions` carries no registry, so C-B2 hoists that construction to one shared instance created in the composition root and injected into both the sessionserver and the `ScrubReporter`, mirroring how the single `slothealth.Tracker` is already threaded into both paths (`sessiondeps.go:255`, `sessionsrv.go:295`, `controlserver.go:149`). Because `SetAdapterLeakedSlots` does an absolute `.Set()` on the `lenny_adapter_leaked_slots{pod_id,pool}` series (`gatewaymetrics.go:1200`), two registries each holding a partial per-pod count would clobber each other on the same series, so a single shared registry is required for the accounting to combine. Keeping a cleanup-time-leaked slot counted in the pod's Redis slot counter, against C-A1's unconditional DECR, is an open decision (see §9).
- **The concurrent trigger reorders `Binder.ReleaseSlot` so the whole-pod recycle `Shutdown` is sent after `SlotClaimer.ReleaseSlot` has patched the claim to `recycling` and armed the missing-report timeout, and before the adapter connection is closed.** Today the adapter connection is closed (`slotbinder.go:499`) before occupancy zero is even computed (the counter decrement is in `SlotClaimer.ReleaseSlot`, `slotclaimer.go:669`). `SlotClaimer.ReleaseSlot` returns a disposition signal so `Binder.ReleaseSlot` can send `ShutdownRecycle` versus `ShutdownSlot`. This mirrors session-mode ordering: patch the claim, arm the timer, then send the recycle `Shutdown`.
- **A failed or crashed last slot retires the pod rather than triggering the whole-pod scrub-and-reuse.** This matches §5.2:453 ("a session that ends in failure or a crash always retires its pod regardless of recycle settings") and 0031's session-mode predicate `result.Recycle && disposition != dispositionFailed`. The concurrent slot-release path carries no per-slot disposition today (`pkg/gateway/session/executor/pod.go:317-318` calls `ReleaseSlot` with no disposition), so the slot's clean-completion-versus-failure signal is threaded through `ReleaseSlot` → `SlotClaimer.ReleaseSlot` and gates the recycle branch.
- **Fail-closed posture is preserved and never weakened.** Until this lands, a concurrent recycling pod at occupancy zero is retired by the missing-report timeout (the current posture). A concurrent `vm-restart` profile with no concrete restarter stays fail-closed through 0031's `ErrNoRestarter` withhold, and cross-tenant reuse is moot because poolstore rejects `allowCrossTenantReuse` on concurrent pools. A leaked last slot still recycles-or-retires under the existing gateway disposition; the leak report is additive to the ledger.

## 3. The two boundaries, the reorder, and config delivery

**The concurrent boundary is a distinct release path.** Session-mode occupancy zero runs through `Binder.Release`, which 0031 wired to patch the claim `recycling`, arm the missing-report timeout, and send the recycle `Shutdown` before closing the adapter. The concurrent boundary runs through `Binder.ReleaseSlot` → `SlotClaimer.ReleaseSlot`. `ReleaseSlot` sends a per-slot `ShutdownSlot` and closes the adapter connection first (`slotbinder.go:498-499`), and `SlotClaimer.ReleaseSlot` decrements the Redis slot counter and, on occupancy zero, patches the claim and arms the timer afterward (`slotclaimer.go:669-704`). The whole-pod scrub can never be triggered on a connection the gateway has already closed, and the last-slot recycle decision is not known until the counter decrement runs. C-A1 reorders `Binder.ReleaseSlot` so the claimer runs first and returns the occupancy-zero disposition, then the adapter call branches on that disposition, then the connection closes.

**The last-slot recycle predicate matches session-mode.** `SlotClaimer.ReleaseSlot` already gates its recycle branch on `remaining == 0 && recycle` (`slotclaimer.go:674-704`). C-A1 adds the clean-terminal condition: a failed or crashed last slot deletes the claim (retire) rather than patching it to `recycling`, matching §5.2:453 and 0031's `disposition != dispositionFailed`. The slot's terminal disposition is threaded from `PodExecutor.Release` (`executor/pod.go:305-319`) and from `rollbackBinding` (`start.go:2361-2374`), which already carries the `failed` disposition on the exclusive path.

**The adapter runs the scrub outside the early-returning slot branch.** The `Shutdown` handler early-returns into `shutdownSlot` on any slot-qualified request (`session.go:226-227`). On the last-slot recycle `Shutdown` the adapter tears the last slot down (so its runtime, tree, and `slotId` are released and, per C-B1, its `ReportSessionScrub` fires) and then runs 0031's `startPodScrub` for the whole-pod scrub and `ReportPodScrub`. This is the "0031 running the scrub outside the early-returning slot branch" that 0031's Non-goals describe.

**Per-slot cleanup fires on every slot release.** (B) is independent of the recycle boundary: `ReportSessionScrub(released|leaked)` fires on every slot completion or failure, siblings-remain and last-slot alike. On the last slot it precedes (A)'s whole-pod scrub. The per-slot cleanup timeout `max(cleanupTimeoutSeconds / maxConcurrentSessions, 5)` bounds the workspace-tree removal and process-group kill; a teardown that exceeds the budget or errors classifies the slot `leaked` and leaves it counted, and the pod continues (`spec/05:541`).

**Config delivery.** Both boundaries need configuration the adapter does not hold. For (A) the last-slot recycle `Shutdown` needs `cleanupCommands`, `cleanupTimeoutSeconds`, `scrubProfile`, and the pod's `SandboxName`, which 0031 folds onto `PoolMatch` and the `BindResult` for the session-mode (`exclusiveBindRequest`) path only and explicitly leaves the concurrent `slotBindRequest` unpopulated. C-W1 extends that plumbing to the concurrent bind result, reusing 0031's existing `RecycleScrub` sub-message with no new wire surface. For (B) the adapter needs the pod identity (the `agent_pod_state` row key, which equals `SandboxName`, which equals the pod's `metadata.name` per `agentsandbox.go:205`) and the inputs to the per-slot cleanup formula on every slot release. C-W1 delivers `pod_id` once at pod creation through the controller pod-spec builder (`pkg/controller/sandbox/podspec/podspec.go`), where the adapter container's args and env are assembled today, as a downward-API env `fieldRef` on `metadata.name`, a per-pod value the controller knows and one the builder sits alongside its `--runtime-uid` injection (`podspec.go:529`). The per-slot cleanup inputs `cleanupTimeoutSeconds` and `maxConcurrentSessions` are per-pool `SessionPolicy` fields owned by the gateway poolstore and deliberately excluded from every CRD (§4.6.3 ownership: the CRD `SessionPolicy` carries only the `Recycle` block, `pkg/apis/lenny/v1alpha1/sandboxtemplate_types.go:28-34`, and the `PoolScalingController` Postgres→CRD projection drops the session-mode sizing knobs, `pkg/controller/poolscaling/poolstoresource.go:117-123`, with `sessionPolicyToCRD` at `:145-159` projecting only `ScrubProfile` and `AcknowledgeMicrovmResidualState`). The Sandbox reconciler reads only CRD objects (`pkg/controller/sandbox/controller.go:322,:329`) and has no DB handle, so the controller cannot source them. The gateway holds the poolstore and already threads `maxConcurrentSessions` onto the concurrent `SlotBindRequest` from the poolstore-sourced `PoolMatch` (`start.go:1918`), so C-W1 delivers both values to the adapter on the existing slot-bind `StartSessionRequest`, and the adapter caches them pod-globally on the `Server`. The adapter applies the `max(.../.., 5)` floor locally per §5.2:541.

## 4. Proposed changes

### C-A1. Thread the last-slot occupancy-zero disposition from `SlotClaimer.ReleaseSlot` to `Binder.ReleaseSlot` and reorder so the whole-pod recycle `Shutdown` fires on a clean last-slot drain

**Target:** `pkg/gateway/podlifecycle/podclaim/slotclaimer.go` (`SlotClaimer.ReleaseSlot`, lines 655-711), `pkg/gateway/podlifecycle/podsession/slotbinder.go` (`Binder.ReleaseSlot`, lines 494-515), `pkg/gateway/session/executor/pod.go` (`PodExecutor.Release` slot branch, lines 305-319), and `pkg/gateway/sessionserver/start.go` (`rollbackBinding`, lines 2361-2374).

**Anchor and change (`slotclaimer.go`):** Change `SlotClaimer.ReleaseSlot` to accept the slot's terminal disposition and to return a typed outcome rather than a bare `error`, so the caller can branch the adapter call. On `remaining > 0` return the siblings-remain outcome. On `remaining == 0 && recycle && !failed` run `WriteRecyclingStatus` and `OnRecycling` as today and return the recycling outcome. On `remaining == 0 && (!recycle || failed)` `DeleteClaim` as today (a failed last slot on a recycling pool retires, `spec/05:453`) and return the retired outcome.

```go
// SlotReleaseOutcome is the occupancy-zero disposition SlotClaimer.ReleaseSlot
// returns so Binder.ReleaseSlot can send the whole-pod recycle Shutdown
// (ShutdownRecycle) versus the per-slot ShutdownSlot. spec: §5.2 (whole-pod
// scrub on occupancy zero), §5.2:453 (failed/crashed session always retires).
type SlotReleaseOutcome int

const (
	SlotSiblingsRemain SlotReleaseOutcome = iota // remaining > 0; the claim stays.
	SlotRecycling                                // clean last-slot drain of a recycling pool: claim patched recycling, timer armed.
	SlotRetired                                  // last slot on a non-recycling pool, or a failed last slot: claim deleted.
)

// failed is the slot's terminal disposition: a failed or crashed last slot
// retires the pod rather than recycling it, mirroring the session-mode
// predicate result.Recycle && disposition != dispositionFailed. spec: §5.2:453.
func (c *SlotClaimer) ReleaseSlot(ctx context.Context, sandboxName, sessionID string, recycle, failed bool) (SlotReleaseOutcome, error) {
	// ... counter guard and DECR unchanged ...
	if remaining > 0 {
		return SlotSiblingsRemain, nil
	}
	if recycle && !failed {
		// WriteRecyclingStatus + OnRecycling exactly as today.
		return SlotRecycling, nil
	}
	// DeleteClaim exactly as today (non-recycling pool, or a failed last slot).
	return SlotRetired, DeleteClaim(ctx, c.Client, c.Namespace, sandboxName)
}
```

**Anchor and change (`slotbinder.go`):** Reorder `Binder.ReleaseSlot` so the claimer runs before the adapter call, and branch the adapter call on the returned outcome. Release credentials first, then call `claimer.ReleaseSlot` to learn the outcome without closing the adapter yet, then send `ShutdownRecycle` on `SlotRecycling` (so the last slot is torn down and the whole-pod scrub runs) or `ShutdownSlot` otherwise, then `Close`.

```go
func (b *Binder) ReleaseSlot(ctx context.Context, result *BindResult, disposition string) error {
	b.releaseCredentials(result.SessionID)
	claimer := &podclaim.SlotClaimer{Client: b.Client, Namespace: b.Namespace, Counter: b.SlotCounter, RecycleBoundary: b.RecycleBoundary}
	failed := disposition == dispositionFailed
	outcome, err := claimer.ReleaseSlot(ctx, result.SandboxName, result.SessionID, result.Recycle, failed)
	// The adapter call runs AFTER the claim is patched recycling and the timer
	// is armed, and BEFORE the connection closes, mirroring session-mode
	// Binder.Release ordering. spec: §5.2 (whole-pod scrub trigger).
	if result.Adapter != nil {
		if outcome == podclaim.SlotRecycling {
			// The last slot's teardown AND the §5.2 whole-pod scrub: the recycle
			// Shutdown carries the slot_id for the slot teardown and the RecycleScrub
			// sub-message for the whole-pod scrub. Reuses 0031's ShutdownRecycle,
			// extended to carry slot_id.
			_, _ = result.Adapter.ShutdownRecycle(ctx, result.SessionID, result.SlotID, adapterclient.RecycleScrub{
				PodID:                 result.SandboxName,
				CleanupCommands:       result.CleanupCommands,
				CleanupTimeoutSeconds: result.CleanupTimeoutSeconds,
				ScrubProfile:          result.ScrubProfile,
			})
		} else {
			_, _ = result.Adapter.ShutdownSlot(ctx, result.SessionID, result.SlotID)
		}
		result.Adapter.Close()
	}
	return err
}
```

`ReleaseSlotReservation` (`slotbinder.go:473-480`), which calls the claimer with `recycle=false` for a slot-count rollback after a failed bind, passes `failed=true` and ignores the returned outcome; it never recycles.

**Anchor and change (`executor/pod.go` and `start.go`):** Thread the terminal disposition into `ReleaseSlot`. `PodExecutor.Release` (`executor/pod.go:317-318`) already receives `disposition`; pass it: `return e.binder.ReleaseSlot(ctx, bind, string(disposition))`. `rollbackBinding` (`start.go:2367`) passes `"failed"`, matching the exclusive path it already gives `Binder.Release`.

**Rationale:** This is the gateway half of (A). Today `Binder.ReleaseSlot` sends `ShutdownSlot` and closes the adapter (`slotbinder.go:498-499`) before `SlotClaimer.ReleaseSlot` computes occupancy (the counter decrement at `slotclaimer.go:669`), so the whole-pod scrub can never be triggered on the connection the gateway just closed. `SlotClaimer.ReleaseSlot` returns the occupancy-zero disposition, and it gates the recycle branch on a clean last-slot drain per §5.2:453. The reorder makes the concurrent path structurally match session-mode `Binder.Release` (patch claim `recycling`, arm timer, then send the recycle `Shutdown`), keeping the missing-report timeout the fail-closed backstop. `ShutdownRecycle` reuses 0031's C-A3 variant, extended so the last-slot recycle `Shutdown` carries the `slot_id` for the slot teardown alongside the `RecycleScrub` sub-message.

**Rationale:** This is the adapter half of (A). The `Shutdown` handler early-returns into `shutdownSlot` on any slot-qualified request (`session.go:226-227`), before 0031's recycle branch. On the last-slot recycle `Shutdown` the adapter tears the last slot down (so its runtime, tree, and `slotId` are released and, per C-B1, `ReportSessionScrub` fires) and then runs 0031's `startPodScrub` for the whole-pod scrub and `ReportPodScrub`. `startPodScrub`, `scrubConfig`, `scrubOutcome`, and the `ErrNoRestarter` withhold are 0031's; this change adds no scrub logic, only the invocation from the concurrent last-slot path. A concurrent `vm-restart` profile with a nil restarter stays fail-closed through 0031's `ErrNoRestarter` withhold, and cross-tenant reuse is moot because poolstore rejects `allowCrossTenantReuse` on concurrent pools.

### C-B1. Emit `ReportSessionScrub(released|leaked)` on every concurrent slot cleanup, bounded by the per-slot cleanup timeout

**Target:** `pkg/adapter/slotsession.go` (`shutdownSlot` lines 63-88, `releaseSlot` lines 94-104), `pkg/adapter/slot.go` (`removeSlotTree` / `RemoveTree`, line 133), and `pkg/adapter/server.go` / `pkg/adapter/gatewaylink.go` (retain the `gatewaycontrol` `Client`'s `ReportSessionScrub` on the `Server` as a `SessionScrubReporter`, alongside 0031's `PodScrubReporter`).

**Anchor and change:** In the slot-teardown path, bound the workspace-tree removal and process-group kill by a per-slot deadline computed as `max(cleanupTimeoutSeconds / maxConcurrentSessions, 5)` seconds (`spec/05:541`; the 5s floor is enforced adapter-side). Capture the outcome: `SessionScrubReleased` when the tree is removed and the process group killed within the budget, `SessionScrubLeaked` when the removal times out or errors (leave the slot counted; the pod continues per §5.2:541). After `releaseSlot` deregisters the slot, emit over the retained emitter.

```go
	outcome := gatewaycontrol.SessionScrubReleased
	if leaked {
		outcome = gatewaycontrol.SessionScrubLeaked
	}
	// The RPC context and the gateway→adapter connection are gone once the slot
	// Shutdown returns and Binder closes; report over the separate GatewayControl
	// link on a background context. podID and the cleanup budget come from C-W1.
	// spec: §5.2:541 (ReportSessionScrub per slot); §6.2:151 (leaked feeds the
	// unhealthy-threshold ledger and the gauge).
	if err := s.SessionScrubReporter.ReportSessionScrub(context.Background(), s.PodID, sessionID, slotID, outcome); err != nil {
		slog.Error("adapter: ReportSessionScrub failed", "pod", s.PodID, "slot", slotID, "err", err)
	}
```

- **A synchronous scrub inside the `Shutdown` response, or a new Gateway → Adapter RPC.** The whole-pod scrub stays asynchronous and bounded by the existing missing-report timeout, and both reports reuse the existing `Shutdown` RPC and the existing GatewayControl `ReportSessionScrub` / `ReportPodScrub` emitters.

## 8. Resolved in adversarial review

Adversarial review rounds populate this section as they run. The following scoping revisions are already baked into the staged changes.

## 9. Open decisions for review

- **Session-mode `sessionsServed` increment and the `maxSessionsPerPod` trigger.** `IncrementSessionsServed` is called only from `RecordSessionScrub` (the `ReportSessionScrub` handler, `scrubreport_server.go:389-390`). With no adapter `ReportSessionScrub` caller in production, `sessionsServed` is never incremented even at `maxConcurrentSessions: 1`, so `recycle.maxSessionsPerPod` never fires. §5.2:451 says the per-slot cleanup runs on "every session release" uniformly across session-mode configurations, which would require a session-mode `ReportSessionScrub` (empty `slot_id`) caller as well. This proposal scopes (B) to the concurrent per-slot cleanup (§5.2:541). Should the session-mode `ReportSessionScrub` caller, and thus the `maxSessionsPerPod` trigger, be folded into this proposal or filed as a third follow-on?
- **Keeping a cleanup-time-leaked slot counted in the pod's Redis slot counter.** §5.2:541 and §6.2:151 require a leaked slot to remain counted in the Redis slot counter so the gateway does not over-assign a new slot onto the leaked slot's unreleased resources. C-A1 decrements that counter unconditionally in `SlotClaimer.ReleaseSlot` (`slotclaimer.go:669`), synchronously at release, while the leak is determined asynchronously by the adapter after teardown and reported later through `ReportSessionScrub`. Neither obvious mechanism is clean: re-incrementing the counter on the async leak report can over-count if a new slot was assigned in the interval, and gating the DECR on the cleanup outcome requires the outcome synchronously, which the adapter cannot provide from a separate process. C-B2 keeps the leaked slot counted in the `slotstate.Registry` (`MarkLeaked` retains it in `active_slots` until pod termination), which gates assignment on the gateway side; whether that is the operative "counted" mechanism §6.2:151 intends, or whether the Redis counter must also hold the slot (and by which mechanism), is unresolved and may need a spec clarification through the pipeline. This proposal ships C-B2's registry and gauge accounting and defers the Redis-counter reconciliation.
- **Whether §4.7 or §5.2 want a one-line note documenting the delivery of `pod_id` and the per-slot cleanup budget to the adapter.** C-W1 delivers `pod_id` as a pod-lifetime constant at pod creation (the downward-API `metadata.name` env) and the per-slot cleanup budget on the slot-bind `StartSessionRequest`, parallel to how 0031's S-A1 documents the recycle `Shutdown`'s carried parameters. If a note is wanted, this becomes a small spec edit through the pipeline rather than fully implementation-only; if the delivery is a pure implementation detail below the spec's altitude, no note is added. The staged changes assume no note is required.
